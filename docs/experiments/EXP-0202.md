# EXP-0202 — Gate/Up-lifetime prefetch of first Down direct-W4 group

## Decision and purpose

EXP-0201 is the fastest evidence-valid pending W4U8 decode candidate at
43.474 token/s. Down remains the largest unoptimized projection tail at about
4.18 ms/token. Its valid direct-n contract uses two output tiles per HMX
command and begins with a serialized packed-W4 plus bias transfer. Earlier
experiments proved that a four-tile live Down group is not executable, so this
experiment does not change the HMX batch.

EXP-0202 starts only the already-valid first two-tile Down transfer into the
same known compressed-weight and bias slots while the interleaved Gate/Up and
asynchronous SwiGLU stream are active. Down consumes that exact prefetched
group, then resumes the unchanged two-slot DMA/HMX ring.

## Controlled implementation

One runtime-selectable binary compares EXP-0201 without and with the Down
cross-prefetch. The candidate may change only logical-M1 prefetch lifetime,
first-group consumption, telemetry and experiment tools. It must not allocate
VTCM, enlarge the live Down group, alter the two-output HMX command, or use an
address not already validated by the existing Down ring.

Weights, scales, qparams, arithmetic, Gate/Up/SwiGLU scheduling, QKV,
Attention, O, residual/RMSNorm, cache, LM head, M64 prefill, F16F16, W4F16 and
the no-QNN architecture remain immutable.

## Gates

Correctness requires byte-exact Down, hidden, cache, selected-code and token
outputs. Physical evidence requires exactly 8 MiB VTCM, zero timed
intermediate DDR, zero spill/fill, one FastRPC per pass, one HMX owner, no QNN,
zero decode expansion, identical packed-W4 bytes, DMA descriptors, HMX
commands and tile pairs, and one started and consumed Down prefetch per layer.

A five-pair short gate must lower Down wall and increase complete directly
measured decode token/s. A passing candidate completes ten rotated 193-step
pairs. M64 prefill is preservation-only. Adoption remains pending explicit
user promotion.
