## New hypothesis

EXP-0041 leaves an FP16 Attention island inside the otherwise asymmetric-U8
block. Q and K are normalized and rotated in U8, after which Q, K and V are
expanded to FP16. QK and AV use FP16 HMX, Softmax uses standard FP16/HVX, and
the result is quantized back to U8. EXP-0042 replaces that complete island,
rather than substituting an isolated Softmax callback.

The previous mllm/QNN custom-Softmax route could not control the adjacent QK
and AV HMX work or the compiler's cross-operator schedule. This standalone
runtime owns QK, Softmax, AV, VTCM buffers, HMX/HVX ordering and GQA task
publication. It is therefore a materially new execution hypothesis rather
than a retry of the rejected isolated-custom-op approach.

## Fixed scope and numerical contract

- The Execution Unit remains the real Qwen3-1.7B layer-14 complete block at
  `M=64`, with sixteen Q heads, eight KV heads and head dimension 128. KV-cache
  and decode shapes remain deferred.
- Declared Q, K, V and Attention-output boundaries remain asymmetric U8. K and
  V may be requantized inside VTCM into zero-centred S8 carriers solely for the
  signed HMX right operand. This does not redefine the model activation
  contract as symmetric S8.
- Integer QK uses U8 Q and S8 K. Its dynamically generated HMX bias accounts
  for Q zero point and the per-position S8 K sums. Integer AV uses U8
  probability with zero point zero and S8 V. HMX conversion produces the
  declared U8 Attention output directly.
- QK output is an internal U8 log2-score grid. Candidate fractional grids
  `F=3` and `F=4` bake Q/K scales, `1/sqrt(128)` and `1/ln(2)` into the HMX
  accumulator conversion. Saturation counts and score error are reported.
- The primary Softmax candidate uses tile-wise maximum tracking, four-bit
  log2 exponent codes, integer row sums and approximate log-based division.
  Causally masked elements are forced to exact zero rather than represented by
  the smallest nonzero exponent code.
- Device arithmetic is checked byte-for-byte against an independent host
  integer Implementation Reference. The current EXP-0041 FP16 Attention island
  is an accuracy diagnostic. Full-model accuracy, perplexity and question
  answering are not gates.

## Physical contract

- One Host-to-DSP FastRPC invocation, one HMX owner and an exact 8 MiB VTCM
  Prepared Runtime Session are retained.
- Scores, exponent codes, row statistics, probabilities and Attention output
  remain VTCM state. Intermediate DDR, spill/fill, a QNN path, hidden Host
  execution and persistent pre-expanded tensors are forbidden.
- The eight GQA groups are independent tasks. Each owns two Q heads and one
  K/V head. HMX executes one group's QK or AV while HVX workers pack K/V or run
  another group's Softmax. Generation-tagged ring slots prevent the HMX owner
  and HVX producers from reusing live storage.
- Probability is generated directly in integer-HMX activation-tile layout.
  A complete row-major probability tensor may not be introduced merely for
  compatibility.

## Milestones and gates

Stage A constructs a single-GQA-group micrograph for U8-by-S8 QK and AV. It
must prove the intended integer HMX instructions, dynamic zero-point
correction, exact host-reference output, fixed-VTCM residency and zero
intermediate DDR before the fused Softmax path is admitted.

Stage B implements the tile-wise log2 Softmax and fuses its U8 probability
carrier directly into AV. Hardware output must remain byte-exact with the
integer reference. Error against standard FP16 Attention is reported by
probability row sum, maximum and mean error, output U8 LSB error and cosine,
but does not stop implementation.

Stage C integrates eight GQA tasks into the complete W4U8 block. Formal
comparison uses repeat one and repeat ten against the unchanged EXP-0041 FP16
Attention-island control. Local adoption requires strict reduction of both the
complete Attention interval and complete Host wall at both repeat counts while
all physical gates pass. A performance failure is retained as valid negative
evidence and does not change the selected baseline.
