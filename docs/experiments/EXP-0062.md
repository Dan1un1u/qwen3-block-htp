# EXP-0062 — Share preconverted RoPE coefficients across Q/K tasks

## Parent and scope

EXP-0061 is the direct source control. Keep its exact HVX square reduction,
16-row paired reciprocal-square-root chunks, all U8 qparams, tensor layouts,
projection schedules, integer Attention, residual equations, and MLP path.
Change only where the existing FP16 RoPE cosine and sine values are converted
to SF32 for Q/K Norm plus RoPE.

## Hypothesis

The 64 RoPE rows are common to all 24 Q/K heads, but the twelve paired tasks
currently repeat the same FP16-to-SF32 conversion independently. Preconverting
each row once into a 64 KiB read-only VTCM cache should eliminate eleven of
twelve copies of that conversion work. The cache can overlay the unused
Attention-projection buffer during Q/K preparation, so the arena peak and all
DDR traffic remain unchanged.

## Candidate and gates

Control and candidate must remain selectable in one binary. The cache must be
fully prepared before paired Q/K workers consume it; its region must not
overlap numerical-audit scratch or per-worker reciprocal-square-root scratch.
The final output and QK/probability/AV audit boundaries must remain byte-exact
to EXP-0061 and the independent reference. Exactly 8 MiB VTCM, zero
intermediate DDR, zero spill/fill, one FastRPC execution unit, one HMX owner,
and no QNN remain mandatory.

At repeat one and repeat ten, aggregate Q/K Norm plus RoPE work, the QKV
critical-path ledger, and complete Host wall must strictly improve in both
ordinary and paired medians. Seven interleaved paired rounds and PC-027/PC-028
complete profiling closure are required.
