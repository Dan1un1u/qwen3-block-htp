# EXP-0212 — Down8-tail prefetch of next-layer Q32 carrier

## Decision and purpose

EXP-0203 previously moved the next layer's first Q transfer under the final
Down2 HMX command. QKV became faster but Down became equally slower because
the HMX tail was shorter than the transfer. EXP-0212 retests that physical
hypothesis only after EXP-0211 changed Down to batch8. The available final-HMX
window is now four times the old Down2 work, while the Q32 packed-W4 transfer
is twice the former Q16 transfer. The relative overlap opportunity therefore
improves by approximately two times.

For layers 0 through 26, the candidate starts the next layer's first Q32
packed-W4 transfer during the current layer's final Down8 HMX command. The
following QKV ring consumes that exact carrier and transfers only its normal
bias. The descriptor replaces the original Q weight descriptor rather than
duplicating it.

## Gates and decision state

Correctness requires byte-exact hidden state, cache, selected codes and
tokens. Physical gates remain exact 8 MiB VTCM, zero timed intermediate DDR,
zero spill/fill, one FastRPC, one HMX owner, no QNN, zero explicit W4
expansion, identical total packed-W4 bytes, DMA descriptors, HMX commands and
tile pairs, plus exactly 27 started and consumed cross-layer Q carriers per
decode token. Five rotated pairs must improve QKV wall and full decode token/s
before ten formal pairs. The consecutive local-win/E2E-regression streak is
zero at entry.

Approved and registered on 2026-09-05 under the continuous decode-loop
authorization. The implementation is source commit
`1c3479db4089cc871192a524787b1dfd122ed1cd`.

The four-step audit and five rotated 193-step pairs were valid and byte exact.
All physical invariants passed: exactly 27 cross-layer starts, consumes and
overlap schedules per decode token; 989 direct-N HMX commands; 1830 weight DMA
descriptors; identical W4 bytes and HMX tile pairs; exact 8 MiB VTCM; zero
timed intermediate DDR, zero spill/fill, one FastRPC, one HMX owner, no QNN
and zero explicit W4 expansion.

The local target improved strongly: QKV plus Q/K preparation fell from
2485.320 to 2004.608 us/token, or 23.98% faster. However, Down increased from
3336.066 to 3823.261 us/token because the implementation waited for the
prefetch at the end of Down. Full decode therefore changed from 46.220925 to
46.202241 tok/s (-0.0404%), with only one of five candidate pairs faster.
This is a direct local-win/E2E-regression result: the wait moved from QKV into
Down rather than leaving the critical path.

Evidence is retained at

The candidate is rejected, no formal run is warranted, and no Selected

the DMA active across the Down boundary and complete it only at the next QKV
consumer, extending overlap across residual, metadata and Input RMSNorm.
