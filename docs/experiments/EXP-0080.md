# EXP-0080 — Cross-projection W4U8 Q-to-K-to-V first-batch prefetch

## Parent and scope

EXP-0079 is the direct source parent and control. Preserve its persistent MLP
HVX ownership, dependency-driven Attention stream, numerical operators,
qparams, tensor layouts, exact 8 MiB VTCM, zero intermediate DDR, one FastRPC
Execution Unit and one HMX owner.

## Hypothesis

The W4U8 QKVO pipeline double-buffers DMA, HVX expansion and HMX only within one
projection. Q, K and V each begin with an exposed first-batch weight-and-bias
DMA wait. Starting K's unchanged first four-output-tile batch while Q's final
HMX command is active, and V's first batch while K's final HMX command is
active, should hide two projection cold starts. The following projection adopts
the completed batch and continues its unchanged two-slot schedule.

## Gates

The candidate may change only submission time and ownership handoff of K and V
first-batch weight-and-bias DMA descriptors. Exactly two cross-projection
prefetches and two adoptions are required per block. Q/K/V arithmetic, four-tile
batch size, alternating slots, W4 expansion, HMX commands, Q/K preparation,
Attention, O projection, MLP and all other stages remain unchanged. Failure must
drain any active prefetch and fail the invocation.

Final output and audited boundaries must remain byte-exact to EXP-0079 and the
independent reference. Weight bytes, total DMA descriptors, 176 HMX commands,
49,408 tile pairs, exact 8 MiB VTCM, zero intermediate DDR, zero spill/fill,
one FastRPC Execution Unit, one HMX owner and no QNN dependency are mandatory.
At repeat one and repeat ten, QKV projection ledger time and complete Host wall
must strictly improve in ordinary and seven-round paired medians. PC-027 and
PC-028 closure are required before any adoption decision.

## Result

Formal evidence was collected in
`D:/llm_exp/results/qwen3-block-htp/exp0080/20260830T124910Z_467ef2e7195d_formal`.
The candidate executed exactly two cross-projection prefetches and two
adoptions per block. Final output and all audited boundaries remained
byte-exact to EXP-0079 with zero mismatches and zero maximum LSB error. The
candidate preserved 25,444,352 weight bytes, 512 weight-DMA descriptors, 176
HMX commands, 49,408 U8xS8 tile pairs, the exact 8 MiB VTCM grant, zero
intermediate DDR traffic, zero spill/fill, one FastRPC Execution Unit, one HMX
owner and no QNN dependency.

The timing gate failed. At repeat one, ordinary QKV ledger time changed from
8,147 to 8,161 ticks (+0.172%) and the seven-round paired median changed by
+0.353%; Host wall changed from 2,379,114 to 2,369,063 ns (-0.422%) but the
paired median regressed by +0.372%. At repeat ten, QKV ledger time changed from
8,139.3 to 8,147.0 ticks (+0.095%; paired -0.068%) and Host wall changed from
1,955,921.9 to 1,953,015.6 ns (-0.149%; paired -0.098%). The candidate reduced
QKVO prefetch wait by 2.889% at repeat ten, but increased QKVO HMX lifetime by
0.255% and projection HMX wait by 17.347%. The first-batch DMA cold start is
therefore not the QKV critical path under this schedule; earlier submission
extends slot/queue lifetime without reducing the additive QKV interval.

## Decision

The local gate is **FAIL** and adoption status is **rejected**. EXP-0079 remains
the Provisional Source Parent and latest eligible W4U8 candidate. The Selected
W4U8 Baseline is unchanged because only the user may promote it.
