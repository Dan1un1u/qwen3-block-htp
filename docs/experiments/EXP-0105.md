# EXP-0105 — Dual-head U8 Softmax reduction fusion

## Decision and motivation

The accepted EXP-0084 Softmax vector already carries two 64-element heads, but
its maximum, log2-weight sum and probability sum are each reduced by two
separate helper calls. Each helper walks the full reduction tree before the
second head begins. This creates serial loop and call boundaries even though
the two heads are independent and use the same valid-row boundary.

EXP-0105 tests paired reduction helpers that advance both heads at every HVX
tree level. The maximum path unpacks the two 64-byte heads to independent U16
vectors and performs six interleaved reduction levels. Weight and probability
sums likewise advance two independent word reductions in one helper.

## Fixed contract and gates

This is a clean sibling from accepted EXP-0084. QK requantization, the log2
approximation, causal mask, exponent codes, LUT generation and lookup,
probability rounding, score/probability carrier layout, Softmax task slicing,
worker count, QK/AV HMX, projections, residuals, MLP, DMA graph and HMX ownership
remain fixed. Control and candidate coexist in one binary.

Static evidence must show the dual-head maximum, weight-sum and probability-
sum paths with no vector stack access and no carrier-layout change. Audit-on
control and candidate must be byte-exact at score, probability, AV and final
output with zero mismatches and zero maximum LSB.

Both cells request/acquire exactly 8 MiB VTCM, retain the 5,306,080-byte peak
plan, one FastRPC, one HMX owner, zero intermediate DDR and spill/fill, and the
same weight bytes, DMA descriptors, HMX commands and 49,408 U8-by-S8 tile pairs.
Five order-rotated rounds at repeat one and repeat ten require aggregate
Softmax work, additive Attention wall and complete Host wall to strictly
improve by ordinary and paired medians at both repeats. PC-027 and PC-028 close
the experiment; a local pass remains pending explicit user adoption.
