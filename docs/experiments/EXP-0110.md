# EXP-0110 — W4F16 QKV specialization factorial

## Decision and scope

The user approved the first Recipe Specialization after the EXP-0109 Public
Common Layer freeze. The experiment is W4F16-only and does not modify the
frozen fair F16F16 or W4U8 configurations. It reopens two valid but previously
cross-recipe-rejected components under their declared conditions: EXP-0108's
Crouton-native `qkv_norms` carrier and EXP-0085's fixed
`q_prefix4_k_all` projection order.

## Factorial

Exactly four cells are allowed: frozen EXP-0109 W4F16 `norms` with Q-major
order; carrier-only `qkv_norms`; order-only Q0-Q3, all K, Q4-Q15, all V; and
the carrier-plus-order combination. There is no additional prefix, group,
batch, worker-count or prefetch-distance search.

The carrier keeps Q/K/V projection output in HMX Crouton order and feeds Q/K
Norm-RoPE, QK and AV without full row-major Q/K/V materialization. The order
candidate changes only projection/readiness order. Neither component may
change weights, arithmetic, qparams, tensor shape, head semantics, Attention,
O projection, MLP, residuals or the frozen public configurations.

## Gates

All cells must preserve output hash `f18b9abbe1487231` and pass the independent
QKV operand audit with zero mismatch and zero maximum LSB. They must request
exactly 8 MiB VTCM, use zero intermediate DDR and spill/fill, one FastRPC
invocation, one HMX owner and no QNN while preserving W4 bytes and HMX tile
work.

Carrier cells must report 128 skipped QKV unpack tiles, 24 direct QK operands,
eight direct AV weights and zero operand mismatches. Prefix cells must publish
exactly Q0-Q3, K0-K7, Q4-Q15 and V0-V7 in that order without delayed or early
head consumption.

A candidate is eligible only if both the QKV-plus-Q/K-Norm-RoPE interval and
complete Host wall strictly improve over control in ordinary and paired
repeat-one and repeat-ten medians. The selected candidate is the lowest
complete Host-wall cell among eligible candidates. Passing creates a W4F16
specialization candidate; it does not change the Frozen Public Baseline
without an explicit user adoption decision.
