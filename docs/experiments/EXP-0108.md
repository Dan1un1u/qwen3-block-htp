# EXP-0108 — Current-baseline common QKV carrier revalidation

## Decision and prior evidence

The user approved a public QKV-carrier experiment after the common MLP test.
EXP-0038 Stage A already tested a Crouton-native QKV handoff on an earlier
accepted FP16 schedule. It improved the declared QKV-to-Attention boundary by
0.16%/1.26% for F16F16 and 4.13%/3.76% for W4F16, but F16F16 repeat-ten Host
wall regressed by 0.57%, so it was not adopted. This experiment does not erase
that negative evidence or claim a new carrier algorithm.

EXP-0108 is a bounded revalidation on the integrated EXP-0106 source and
formal collection protocol. The explicit user approval is the authority to
reopen this one direction. The purpose is to decide whether public carrier
work should now be frozen before recipe-specialized research, not to launch
another open-ended QKV search.

## Candidate

Control uses the accepted `norms` boundary and `gqa_qkv_overlap` schedule.
Candidate adds only the existing QKV Crouton-native carrier to the same Norm
boundaries, exposed as a `qkv_norms` runtime token. Q, K and V HMX output stays
in the project-owned Crouton representation; Q/K Norm-RoPE and GQA consumers
read that representation directly; the row-major Q/K/V materialization is
skipped. Projection order, HMX command granularity, Attention scheduling,
math, weights, qparams, tensor shapes and all other boundaries remain fixed.

F16F16 and W4F16 are direct control/candidate comparisons. W4U8 already owns a
native U8 QKV carrier, so identical W4U8 runs are a parity control rather than
another implementation.

## Gates

The execution unit is the real Qwen3 layer-14 M=64 complete block. Every cell
must preserve byte-exact output and the independent audit, request exactly
8,388,608 VTCM bytes, use zero intermediate DDR and spill/fill, one FastRPC
invocation, one HMX owner and no QNN.

For both F16F16 and W4F16, the combined QKV plus Q/K Norm-RoPE ledger and
complete Host wall must strictly improve in ordinary and paired repeat-one and
repeat-ten medians. W4U8 must remain within one percent of its identical
control. The candidate must report the expected 128 skipped QKV unpack tiles,
24 direct QK operands, eight direct AV weights and zero operand mismatches.
Failure closes public QKV carrier work under the present physical contract;
passing makes it eligible for a user adoption decision but does not
automatically change the Selected Baseline.

## Result

Completed with valid formal evidence on source commit
`79130ef6f02f13c7fc16d6d8d521ec35bb217179`. The candidate was byte-exact and
the physical implementation behaved as intended: three QKV projections stayed
in Crouton form, 128 unpack tiles were skipped, 24 QK operands and eight AV
weights were consumed directly, operand audit mismatches were zero, the fixed
8 MiB VTCM grant was retained, and intermediate DDR/spill remained zero.

The cross-recipe speed gate failed. W4F16 reproduced a real benefit: the
QKV-plus-Q/K-Norm-RoPE interval improved by 6.44% at repeat one and 6.36% at
repeat ten; complete Host wall improved by 0.59% and 1.53%. F16F16 did not:
the declared interval regressed 0.41% at repeat one and improved only 0.43% at
repeat ten, while complete Host wall regressed 2.40% and 0.91%. Paired Host
medians also regressed by 0.85% and 0.91%. This independently reproduces the
essential EXP-0038 finding on the integrated current source.

The identical W4U8 parity cells were noisy at repeat one and therefore also
missed the one-percent parity gate; this does not affect the decisive F16F16
failure. Formal evidence is retained at
`D:/llm_exp/results/qwen3-block-htp/exp0108/20260831T144457Z_79130ef6f02f_formal`.
No baseline changes automatically. The evidence supports freezing QKV
Crouton-carrier work as a public cross-recipe direction under the current
physical contract; a W4F16-only specialization remains a distinct future
decision.

## Adoption decision

On 2026-08-31 the user rejected this carrier as a public cross-recipe plan.
The valid evidence remains available, but the path may be reopened only as an
explicit W4F16 recipe specialization or after approval of a materially new
public physical contract. It is excluded from EXP-0109 consolidation.
