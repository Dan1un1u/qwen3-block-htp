# EXP-0114 — W4F16 Gate/Up post-semaphore join barrier

## Decision and scope

The user authorized a continuing W4F16/W4U8 recipe-specialization loop.
EXP-0114 returns to W4F16 and derives from the EXP-0112 provisional source
parent, selecting the locally passing EXP-0111 `join_only` W4F16 runtime as
its direct control. It changes neither the Frozen Public Baseline nor F16F16,
W4U8, QKV, Attention or Down.

## Hypothesis

EXP-0111 removed every per-region W4-to-FP16 unpack barrier and generation
publication from nonstreamed Gate/Up groups. Every active HVX worker still
signals its `command_done` QuRT semaphore only after all of its VTCM stores,
and the main HMX owner waits on every corresponding semaphore. The shared pool
wait then executes one additional full Hexagon `barrier` before HMX consumes
the group.

QuRT semaphore producer-consumer handoff is expected to provide the visibility
ordering required between the worker and the waiter. EXP-0114 tests whether
the extra trailing full barrier is redundant. This is distinct from deleting
worker publication ordering in EXP-0113: all worker completion signals and all
waits remain intact.

## Fixed boundary and gates

Control is EXP-0111 `join_only`, including relaxed unpack and the ordinary
post-semaphore full barrier. The sole candidate waits all active worker
`command_done` semaphores but skips only that trailing barrier on the W4F16
Gate/Up nonstreamed group path. The ordinary pool wait and every other caller
retain the barrier. Arithmetic, weights, scales, layouts, DMA, HMX commands,
tile work, ring depth, worker count and every other module are fixed.

Both cells must preserve final output and the independent W4F16 expansion
audit byte-for-byte with zero mismatch and zero maximum LSB. They retain exact
8 MiB VTCM, zero intermediate DDR and spill/fill, one FastRPC execution unit,
one HMX owner and no QNN. Static evidence must prove all worker semaphores are
still joined and only the candidate path lacks the trailing full barrier. The
candidate passes only if Gate/Up plus SwiGLU and complete Host wall strictly
improve in ordinary and paired repeat-one and repeat-ten medians over five
rounds. PC-027 and PC-028 are required; no local pass automatically promotes
a baseline.
