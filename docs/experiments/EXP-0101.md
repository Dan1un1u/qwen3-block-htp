# EXP-0101 — Readiness-aware W4U8 Attention scheduler

## Decision and motivation

The accepted EXP-0084 dependency stream uses separate atomic claim counters
for QK groups, row-sliced Softmax tasks and AV groups. A worker claims the next
Softmax or AV task before checking whether its producer dependency is ready,
then spins on that dependency. With six HVX contexts this can park several
workers behind not-yet-ready groups while other runnable work exists. Formal
EXP-0100 control evidence still reports roughly 1.1–1.2k Attention pipeline-
wait ticks per block, while W4U8 Attention remains materially slower than the
W4F16 comparator.

EXP-0101 tests a readiness-aware nonblocking scheduler. Workers may claim only
QK, Softmax-slice or AV work whose dependencies are already published. When no
downstream task is ready they continue advancing unclaimed QK groups instead
of reserving a future task and waiting. The hypothesis is that this preserves
the useful row-sliced parallelism of EXP-0075 while eliminating claim-before-
ready stalls and increasing overlap among QK HMX, Softmax HVX and AV HMX.

## Fixed contract and gates

This is a clean sibling from accepted EXP-0084. Q/K/V/O projections, QK and AV
HMX commands, row-sliced Softmax math, LUT representation, qparams, tensor
bytes, task cardinalities, worker count and VTCM layout remain byte-identical.
The candidate may change only dependency-task arbitration and add compact
telemetry. It may not add a global stage barrier, a second HMX owner, a new
DDR workspace, or a different Softmax approximation.

Audit-on control and candidate must be byte-exact at final output and at the
established Input-RMSNorm, QK, probability and AV boundaries with zero
mismatches and zero maximum LSB. Both must request and acquire exactly 8 MiB
VTCM, use one FastRPC execution unit and one HMX owner, perform zero
intermediate DDR and spill/fill, and preserve weight bytes, DMA descriptors,
HMX command count and 49,408 U8-by-S8 tile pairs.

Candidate telemetry must prove that Softmax and AV tasks are claimed only
after their dependencies are ready, that all 16 Softmax slices and all eight
AV groups execute once, and that no worker exits before all stages complete.
After the audit gate, five order-rotated rounds at repeat one and repeat ten
compare Attention wall, dependency/pipeline wait and complete Host wall.
Attention and complete Host wall must strictly improve by ordinary and paired
medians at both repeat counts. PC-027 and PC-028 close the experiment; any
local pass remains pending explicit user adoption.

## Result

Completed as a valid negative experiment at source commit
`d8a5a0795847f66364acc28efe5b22efed66d85e`.

Static, scheduler, correctness and physical gates passed. The candidate
executes exactly 16 ready-only Softmax slices and eight ready-only AV groups
per block, reports zero predependency claims, and is byte-exact at the final
output and established Input-RMSNorm, QK, probability and AV boundaries.
Both cells request/acquire exactly 8 MiB VTCM, retain the same 5,306,080-byte
peak plan, perform zero intermediate DDR and spill/fill, and preserve one
FastRPC, one HMX owner, 512 weight DMA descriptors, 176 HMX commands and
49,408 U8-by-S8 tile pairs per block.

The speed gate fails decisively. At repeat one, Attention changes +7.1505%
ordinary/+7.5507% paired and complete Host wall changes +0.4066%/+1.2854%.
At repeat ten, Attention changes +6.3511%/+6.2903% and Host wall changes
+0.5383%/+0.4911%. The candidate does reduce HMX queue/pipeline wait by
33.6%–36.0%, Softmax work by about 1.3%–1.5%, QK HMX work by about 3.9%–4.4%
and AV requant work by about 4.3%–5.1%. Those overlapping counters do not
translate into critical-path savings.

The evidence shows that claim-before-ready is not merely wasted spinning. It
pre-distributes work across HVX contexts and keeps later tasks ready to run as
soon as dependencies publish. Ready-only arbitration reduces measured queue
wait but lowers effective cross-worker concurrency; its roughly 2.4k readiness
polls per block add further scheduler work. Therefore `local_gate=fail` and
`adoption_status=rejected`; the Selected Baseline remains EXP-0084.
