# EXP-0077 — Cross-phase W4U8 Gate/Up initial-batch prefetch

## Parent and scope

EXP-0075 is the direct source parent. Preserve its dependency-driven Attention
stream, all numerical operators and qparams, exact 8 MiB VTCM grant, zero
intermediate DDR, one FastRPC Execution Unit, one HMX owner, and the selected
W4U8 Gate/Up and Down physical plans.

## Hypothesis

The W4U8 MLP currently starts its first Gate/Up linked-DMA batch only after the
post-attention residual/RMSNorm has finished and the projection pipeline has
created its workers. The Gate/Up compressed slots do not overlap the residual,
post-norm activation, or Attention-live regions. Starting the same first four
bundle descriptors immediately before the residual/RMSNorm can overlap their
DDR-to-VTCM transfer with useful HVX work. The projection pipeline can then
wait for and publish that already-running batch instead of submitting it again,
reducing Gate/Up cold-start latency without changing weight bytes or compute.

## Gates

The candidate may change only the submission time and ownership handoff for the
first Gate/Up linked-DMA batch. It must preserve the same four descriptors,
source bytes, destination compressed slots, publication barriers, subsequent
DMA chain, W4 expansion, HMX commands, tile pairs, activation/SwiGLU math,
residual math, buffers and outputs. Prefetch failure must drain safely and fail
the invocation; it may not silently execute a different path.

The final output and audited boundaries must remain byte-exact to EXP-0075 and
the independent implementation reference. Requested/acquired VTCM remains
8,388,608 bytes; intermediate DDR and spill/fill remain zero; weight DDR bytes,
total DMA descriptors, 176 HMX commands and 49,408 U8xS8 tile pairs remain
unchanged. At repeat one and repeat ten, complete Host wall and the combined
post-attention-residual plus Gate/Up ledger interval must strictly improve in
ordinary and seven-round paired medians. PC-027 and PC-028 closure are required.
