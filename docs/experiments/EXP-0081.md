# EXP-0081 — Idle Q/K-preparation worker-assisted QKV W4 expansion

## Parent and scope

EXP-0079 is the direct source parent and control. EXP-0080 is a rejected
sibling and its cross-projection DMA prefetch is not inherited. Preserve the
EXP-0079 persistent MLP workers, dependency-driven integer Attention,
arithmetic, qparams, layouts, exact 8 MiB VTCM, zero intermediate DDR, one
FastRPC Execution Unit, one HMX owner and no QNN path.

## Evidence-driven hypothesis

EXP-0080 reduced QKVO prefetch wait without reducing the additive QKV interval.
At repeat ten, QKVO W4-to-S8 expansion still performs about 6,403 aggregate
ticks on the main HVX context while five persistent Q/K Norm-RoPE workers may
be waiting for future head generations. The current batch already overlaps
main-HVX expansion with HMX consumption, but it does not use those temporarily
idle worker contexts for expansion.

The candidate exposes one generation-safe expansion assignment for the current
four-output-tile batch. The main context and eligible persistent Q/K-preparation
workers expand disjoint tiles. A worker may help only while it is waiting for a
not-yet-published Q/K head; it must immediately return to the unchanged
Norm-RoPE scheduler after its assigned tile. No worker count, HMX command,
head-publication order, DMA descriptor or tensor boundary changes.

## Ordered milestones and gates

Stage A enables worker assistance only for Q projection. K, V, O, Attention,
MLP and residual paths remain the EXP-0079 control. Stage A must prove at least
one worker-assisted tile per eligible Q batch and preserve all twelve paired
Q/K preparation tasks. It passes only if QKV additive wall and complete Host
wall strictly improve in ordinary and seven-round paired medians at repeat one
and repeat ten. Failure stops the experiment. Only after Stage A passes may
Stage B apply the same mechanism to K and V.

Final output and audited boundaries must be byte-exact to EXP-0079 and the
independent reference. Weight bytes, 512 DMA descriptors, 176 HMX commands,
49,408 U8xS8 tile pairs, exact 8 MiB VTCM, zero intermediate DDR, zero
spill/fill, one FastRPC Execution Unit, one HMX owner and no QNN dependency are
mandatory. The report must separately expose assisted batches/tiles, worker
expansion work, main expansion work, assistance wait, Q/K preparation work and
join wait. PC-027 and PC-028 closure are required before any adoption decision.

## Formal result

The authoritative seven-round paired run is
`D:/llm_exp/results/qwen3-block-htp/exp0081/20260830T132210Z_7d52d6c3a861_formal`.
An earlier run ending in `20260830T130628Z_065a0e522568_formal` completed device
sampling but its report renderer failed; it is explicitly non-authoritative and
was not reused after the validator fix.

The implementation and physical gates pass. Candidate output and audited
boundaries are byte-exact to EXP-0079 and the independent reference, with zero
mismatches and zero maximum LSB error. The candidate retains 8 MiB requested
and acquired VTCM, zero intermediate DDR and spill/fill, 512 weight-DMA
descriptors, 176 HMX commands, 49,408 U8xS8 tile pairs, one FastRPC invocation,
one HMX owner and no QNN dependency. Eleven Q batches and twenty Q weight tiles
are worker-assisted per block, with only 2.8 assistance-wait ticks at repeat
ten.

The local speed gate fails. At repeat one, QKV falls from 8,169 to 7,871 ticks
(-3.65%; paired -3.61%) and the ordinary Host median falls from 2,376,770 to
2,358,854 ns (-0.75%), but the paired Host median regresses 2.85%. At repeat
ten, QKV falls from 8,184.8 to 7,835.3 ticks (-4.27%; paired -4.30%) and Host
wall falls from 1,958,875 to 1,937,401 ns per block (-1.10%; paired -1.36%).
QKVO expansion falls about 9.09% and its HMX lifetime about 5.50%, but Q/K
preparation pool wait rises about 19.53%. The worker-assistance mechanism thus
accelerates its intended Q projection while borrowing readiness from the next
Attention phase and does not satisfy the strict repeat-one complete-wall gate.

## Decision

EXP-0081 is completed with valid evidence, `local_gate: fail`, and
`adoption_status: rejected`. Stage B for K/V is not started. EXP-0079 remains
the provisional W4U8 source parent and latest locally eligible candidate; the
user-selected W4U8 baseline remains EXP-0055.
