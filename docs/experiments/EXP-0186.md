# EXP-0186 — Session-native V tail with direct native append

## Decision and purpose

EXP-0185 makes seven mutable K tails authoritative in prepared-session VTCM
and proves that the duplicated DDR row journal is unnecessary.  The matching V
path still converts each native projection row into a temporary row-major
buffer, writes that row to the mutable DDR journal, and then copies the same row
into the persistent VTCM native carrier.  Attention already consumes the VTCM
carrier, so both the row-major bounce and DDR journal are redundant inside a
prepared session.

EXP-0186 introduces a versioned session-native V format paired with EXP-0185
K14.  All eight V heads per layer update their persistent VTCM carrier directly
from the existing QKV projection native carrier.  The mutable DDR V-tail bytes
become non-authoritative and receive no per-token writes.  At row 31, the exact
complete carrier is still written into the unchanged immutable segmented DDR
history, preserving long-context consumption and the external sealed ABI.

## Controlled change and gates

The source parent and direct performance control are the accepted EXP-0185
commit `537650293920eb62c3cc49833df613246e8fb0b9`.  Only the V-tail cache ABI,
direct native row update, append telemetry and experiment tools may change.
K cache, QK/Softmax/AV math, qparams, projections, MLP, LM head and M64 prefill
math remain unchanged.

Correctness requires exact consumed AV carriers, hidden hashes, tokens/codes,
34-step segment-seal bytes, padding-poison invariance, 193 independent integer
head checks and the EXP-0163 regression.  Physical gates require exactly 224
fewer mutable V-tail write descriptors and 28,672 fewer cache DDR write bytes
per ordinary decode token, no row-major V bounce for session-native heads,
exactly 8 MiB VTCM, zero timed intermediate DDR, zero spill/fill, one FastRPC,
no QNN and complete timing closure.

Five rotated short pairs must lower cache conversion/update, append DMA and
complete decode Host wall before a ten-pair 193-step formal run.  Only the user
may promote a passing result.

## Decision state

Approved on 2026-09-04 by the standing user instruction to continue the decode
optimization loop.  Source commit
`5a3f81dba0b5730f6002a05186862b73f6dd0f6f` implements the versioned K14/V15
candidate.

The mandatory short gate completed on PJZ110 and stopped the experiment before
the formal 193-step run.  Correctness and the physical contract passed: the
34-step segment boundary, all short-session hidden hashes, selected tokens and
logit codes, and padding-poison invariance were exact.  The candidate removed
exactly 224 cache-DMA descriptors and 28,672 DDR write bytes per ordinary
decode token, retained identical DDR reads and HMX/weight work, and preserved
the 8 MiB VTCM, one-FastRPC, zero-intermediate-DDR, zero-spill/fill and no-QNN
contracts.

The local gate nevertheless failed.  Across five rotated 17-step pairs the
candidate won only three.  Median decode improved from 42,371.566 to
42,237.048 us/token, or from 23.600733 to 23.675897 token/s (+0.318484%), but
QK-Softmax-AV regressed from 3,390.339 to 3,409.261 us/token (-0.555033%).
Carrier update fell from 423.835 to 239.157 us and append DMA from 132.770 to
24.701 us, so the intended local work was removed but did not reliably shorten
the full critical path.  EXP-0186 is therefore evidence-valid and rejected;
EXP-0185 remains the Selected W4U8 Token-Generation Baseline.

Formal evidence is unavailable by design because the contract requires a stop
after fewer than five of five short-pair wins.  Retained short evidence:
`D:/llm_exp/results/qwen3-block-htp/exp0186/20260904T192112_5a3f81d_short`.
The route may reopen only with a new mechanism that fuses the V-tail update
into QKV publication or otherwise hides it without regressing Attention.
