# EXP-0079 — Hybrid persistent-pool W4U8 Down expansion workers

## Parent and scope

EXP-0078 is the direct source parent and control. Preserve its dependency-driven
Attention stream, native U8 residual and norm paths, three persistent Gate/Up
expansion workers, qparams, tensor layouts, Gate/Up and Down physical plans,
exact 8 MiB VTCM, zero intermediate DDR, one FastRPC Execution Unit and one HMX
owner.

## Hypothesis

EXP-0078 proved that reusing already-locked pool workers removes measurable
Gate/Up startup and HMX-ready wait. Down still creates, locks, joins and destroys
six transient HVX expansion threads per block while the five persistent workers
sleep. Dispatching five unchanged Down expansion consumers to the persistent
pool and retaining one transient sixth consumer should preserve the selected
six-way expansion parallelism while removing five-sixths of the per-block
thread and HVX-context lifecycle.

## Gates

The candidate may change only ownership of the six Down HVX expansion
consumers: exactly five persistent-pool workers plus exactly one transient
worker. Requested Down worker count, the DMA producer, expansion queue, task
allocation, publication generations, expanded-slot lifecycle, one serialized
HMX owner, W4 arithmetic, output requantization, Gate/Up implementation and all
other block stages remain unchanged.

Final output and audited boundaries must remain byte-exact to EXP-0078 and the
independent reference. Weight bytes, DMA descriptors, 176 HMX commands, 49,408
tile pairs, exact 8 MiB VTCM, zero intermediate DDR, zero spill/fill, one
FastRPC Execution Unit, one HMX owner and no QNN dependency are mandatory. At
repeat one and repeat ten, Down ledger time and complete Host wall must strictly
improve in ordinary and seven-round paired medians. PC-027 and PC-028 closure
are required before any adoption decision.

## Result

Implementation commit `8296e3de6bf1e22faa8ba3480fa6dc949d5a4e0a`
keeps six Down expansion consumers while replacing five transient thread and
HVX-context lifecycles with five existing persistent pool workers. Formal
evidence is retained at
`D:/llm_exp/results/qwen3-block-htp/exp0079/20260830T123738Z_8296e3de6bf1_formal`.
The candidate records one Down persistent dispatch, five persistent workers and
one transient worker per block; the control records six transient workers.

Final output and all audited boundaries remain byte-exact to EXP-0078, with
zero mismatches and zero maximum LSB error. Weight DDR bytes, 512 DMA
descriptors, 176 HMX commands, 49,408 U8xS8 tile pairs, exact 8 MiB VTCM, zero
intermediate DDR, zero spill/fill, one FastRPC Execution Unit, one HMX owner and
no QNN dependency are unchanged.

At repeat one, Down falls from 6,869 to 6,154 ticks (-10.41%, paired -10.20%)
and complete Host wall falls from 2,426,563 to 2,371,614 ns per block (-2.26%,
paired -2.95%). At repeat ten, Down falls from 6,873.2 to 6,160.8 ticks per
block (-10.36%, paired -10.76%) and complete Host wall falls from 1,997,041.6
to 1,954,026.1 ns per block (-2.15%, paired -2.06%). Down HMX-ready wait falls
5.44% at repeat one and 6.77% at repeat ten.

## Decision

Correctness, physical-contract, hybrid-worker execution and both repeat speed
gates pass. EXP-0079 is locally adoption-eligible and becomes the latest
eligible W4U8 candidate. It does not change the user-selected W4U8 baseline
without explicit promotion.
