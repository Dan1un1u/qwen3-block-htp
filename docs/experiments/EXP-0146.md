# EXP-0146 — W4F16 batch-two cross-projection QKV ring

## Decision and hypothesis

The user approved this experiment on 2026-09-01 as the next W4F16 recipe
specialization. It branches from the selected fastest W4F16 baseline EXP-0140,
commit `e75563457293de9430f8c9d20a04ced6147e4737`.

EXP-0143 and EXP-0144 exposed a large W4U8 QKV critical-path tail caused by
small carrier copies around Q/K Norm-RoPE. Their exact SIMD transpose is not a
W4F16 optimization: EXP-0110 already gives W4F16 a direct Crouton-native QKV
handoff, and the FP16 kernel already uses aligned HVX `vdeal`, `vshuff`, and
`vscatter`. The transferable mechanism is instead the long-lived Q/K/V
producer-consumer pipeline.

The current EXP-0140 W4F16 path executes three projection lifecycles in order.
It overlaps Q/K preparation with published heads and cross-prefetches the next
projection, but it does not retain one bounded DMA-expand-HMX ring across all
of Q, K, and V. The hypothesis is that such a ring can remove lifecycle
boundaries and expose compressed-weight movement gains without changing
arithmetic or delaying head readiness.

## Fixed implementation boundary

The control is the exact EXP-0140 selected candidate. The experimental cell
must preserve its W4 values and scales, FP16 activations, FP16 HMX arithmetic,
batch-two HMX commands, direct Crouton-native QKV carrier, per-head publication,
one HMX owner, Attention, O, MLP, residuals, FastRPC boundary, and complete
block scope. EXP-0126 and EXP-0127 already reject fixed batch-four searches, so
batch width is not a variable here.

The only allowed mechanism is a bounded cross-projection ring that schedules
compressed W4 DMA, W4-to-FP16 expansion, batch-two HMX consumption, and Q/K
Norm-RoPE readiness across Q, K, and V. Ring storage must be a proven
phase-aware overlay inside the exact 8 MiB VTCM grant. Intermediate DDR,
spill/fill, another HMX owner, QNN, changed tensor math, or waiting for a whole
projection before publishing a complete head are hard failures.

## Gates

Before implementation proceeds to hardware, a static capacity audit must show
that every live compressed, expanded, bias/scale, output, Attention, and MLP
region remains non-overlapping in its actual lifetime and that peak allocation
does not exceed 8 MiB.

Both cells must produce byte-exact output `f18b9abbe1487231`, zero W4F16
expansion mismatch, zero QKV operand mismatch, and zero maximum LSB error. They
must request and acquire exactly 8 MiB VTCM, retain zero intermediate DDR and
spill/fill, one FastRPC execution unit, one HMX owner, identical weight bytes,
DMA bytes, HMX tile pairs, carriers and arithmetic, with only the declared ring
topology and wait/command lifecycle allowed to differ.

The local speed gate requires QKV plus Q/K Norm-RoPE and complete Host wall to
strictly improve in ordinary and paired repeat-one and repeat-ten medians. Five
rotated formal rounds, PC-027 and PC-028 are mandatory. A local pass remains a
candidate until the user explicitly adopts or promotes it under PC-029.
