   Keep raw BF16 files untouched. Build under isolated SDK; inspect VTCM/tile
   bounds and reject unsupported paths explicitly.
4. Single-layer hardware prefill and decode with historical KV, then 2-3 layers,
   then full-model text and independent heldout PPL. Freeze fixture definitions
   and numerical thresholds before hardware scoring; retain all failed attempts.
5. Record actual scope and timings. No throughput extrapolation, no quality
   promotion, no W4 or A8 evaluation before this floating port is validated.
