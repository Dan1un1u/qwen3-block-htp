# EXP-0123 — W4F16 Gate/Up persistent expansion workers

## Decision and scope

The user authorized a continuing W4F16/W4U8 recipe-specialization loop and
routine autonomous recovery. EXP-0123 returns to W4F16 and branches from the
EXP-0120 source, which retains the locally passing EXP-0111 W4F16 `join_only`
path and the provisional EXP-0120 W4U8 path. Only W4F16 Gate/Up expansion
worker lifetime and handoff are in scope.

## Evidence and hypothesis

EXP-0111 lowers Gate/Up to 916.7 microseconds and proves that nonstreamed
per-region publication barriers are redundant, but the implementation still
dispatches and joins two HVX expansion workers for every Gate and Up group.
At repeat ten the final streaming join is about 589 ticks and the expansion
pool performs many short semaphore epochs. W4U8 evidence shows that persistent
workers plus a bounded generation ring can remove this class of scheduling
overhead.

The candidate starts the same two W4-to-FP16 expansion workers once for the
complete Gate/Up phase. The main HMX owner publishes bounded group descriptors
after the corresponding packed-weight DMA completes; workers claim only their
unchanged region partitions and publish generation-tagged completion. Main
retains its existing expansion partition and consumes each group in the same
Gate-then-Up order. No additional HMX owner or arithmetic change is allowed.

## Gates

Control and candidate are runtime selectable from one binary. Weight values,
FP16 scales, packed bytes, DMA descriptors and bytes, expansion regions, HMX
commands and tile pairs, Crouton-native SwiGLU, Down, QKV, Attention and all
other modules remain fixed. Candidate telemetry must close persistent worker
dispatch, claimed job, completion, wait and fallback counts.

Final output and the independent W4F16 expansion audit must remain byte-exact.
Exact 8 MiB VTCM, zero intermediate DDR and spill/fill, one FastRPC unit, one
HMX owner, no QNN and unchanged peak VTCM are hard gates. The candidate is
eligible only if Gate/Up and complete Host wall strictly improve in ordinary
and paired repeat-one and repeat-ten medians over five rotated rounds. PC-027
and PC-028 are mandatory. A local pass does not promote a baseline without the
user's explicit decision.
