# EXP-0029 — QK/AV Crouton-packing critical path

## Approval and status

Approved by the user on 2026-08-28 after EXP-0028 restored a strict W4F16
projection and complete-block advantage over the fair F16F16 control. This
experiment starts from EXP-0028 commit
`6ba4e75de485a3070397342b8e4ffe09c8dbe15e` and changes only the shared FP16
Attention operand-packing path. It does not accept EXP-0028, promote a Selected
Baseline, or change EXP-0023's role as the Provisional Source Parent.

## Motivation

At repeat ten, the EXP-0028 W4F16 candidate spends about 152.9K qtimer ticks in
Attention, or 66.3% of the complete block, while all W4F16 projections take
37.35K ticks. The nested exclusive ledger attributes about 145.7K Attention
ticks to QK and AV packing, approximately 95.3% of Attention. QK and AV HMX
execution together take only about 1.49K ticks and standard stable FP16 Softmax
takes about 5.31K ticks.

W4F16 projections are already 14.56% faster than the fair F16F16 projection
control, but the common Attention bottleneck dilutes that advantage to 2.59%
at the complete-block Host boundary. Reducing the shared packing path should
speed both variants while making the existing W4 weight-flow advantage more
visible in the complete block. It is not itself a W4-specific optimization.

## Fixed contract

- The Execution Unit remains one complete real Qwen3-1.7B layer-14 block at
  `M=64` in one FastRPC invocation. Formal timing retains repeat one and repeat
  ten; repeat 28 is not introduced.
- F16F16 keeps EXP-0027's double-buffered batch-two projection schedule. W4F16
  keeps EXP-0028's adaptive 32/96-region pipeline and cross-projection
  prefetch. Their weights, arithmetic, scales, HMX commands, and schedule
  search results are frozen.
- RMSNorm, RoPE mathematics, score scaling, causal mask, standard stable FP16
  Softmax, AV mathematics, SiLU, residuals, and output arithmetic remain
  unchanged. A candidate may change the physical producer/consumer layout at
  a QK or AV boundary only when the same logical values and HMX operand
  contract are preserved.
- The Attention packing implementation and selected Physical Plan are shared
  by F16F16 and W4F16. Variant-specific packing shortcuts are forbidden.
- Every mode requests and receives exactly 8,388,608 VTCM bytes. Q, K, V,
  score, probability, and AV intermediates remain in VTCM. Intermediate DDR,
  spill/fill, extra FastRPC calls, QNN, and QAIRT are hard failures.
- Equivalent-scope Host wall is primary. The Block Timing Ledger and nested
  Attention ledger remain exclusive and closed. Overlapping HMX, HVX, and DMA
  work counters are diagnostic and may not be summed as wall time.

## Ordered milestones

### A — Establish the physical packing contract

Audit the current QK and AV source layouts, Crouton destination address
mapping, bytes moved, scalar versus HVX work, head/GQA-group loop structure,
and HMX command boundary. Add compact counters or sampled timestamps only when
the existing exclusive ledger cannot distinguish packing work from handoff
wait. Do not print inside hot loops.

### B — Direct QK Crouton packing

Implement at least one candidate that reduces complete QK operand construction
without changing QK arithmetic. Prefer HVX vector permutation and direct
stores to the HMX-consumable Crouton layout over scalar gather/scatter or a
second staging tensor. If RoPE output can be written directly into the required
QK consumer layout without changing RoPE mathematics, it may be evaluated as a
declared physical-layout candidate.

### C — Direct AV Crouton packing

Implement at least one candidate that reduces construction of the probability
and V operands used by AV. Prefer producing or transforming the operands once
into the exact HMX-consumable layout. Softmax arithmetic, mask semantics, and
probabilities remain fixed.

### D — Combined complete-block gate

Combine only individually correct candidates. The candidate must preserve the
implementation-reference gates and stable output identity, request the full
8 MiB VTCM grant, retain zero intermediate DDR and one FastRPC invocation, and
close both timing ledgers.

The local speed gate requires the combined candidate to reduce exclusive
Attention ticks and not regress complete-block Host wall for both F16F16 and
W4F16 at repeat one and repeat ten. W4F16 must remain strictly faster than the
fair F16F16 control at both repeat counts. A result that improves only engine
work counters or only one repeat count does not pass.

If neither QK nor AV packing can be reduced without changing the HMX operand
contract or introducing DDR, stop and report the physical limitation rather
than changing Softmax or projection quantization.

## Implementation

The audit found that the source activation packing path was already vectorized,
but both Attention right-hand operands were still constructed by nested scalar
loops. QK packed each K head with scalar scatter into the HMX Crouton layout,
and AV packed the transposed V operand with scalar element copies. Each K/V
head was also repacked for both query heads in its GQA group.

The candidate replaces only these physical packing paths. QK uses one aligned
HVX word-offset vector and `Q6_vscatter_RMVwV` to write directly into the HMX
Crouton carrier. AV loads paired source rows, applies `Q6_W_vshuff_VVR`, and
stores the resulting vectors directly in the transposed Crouton carrier. K and
V are packed once per GQA group and reused by its two query heads. QK HMX,
standard FP16 Softmax, AV HMX, all model mathematics, weights, qparams, and
projection schedules are unchanged.

Independent `qk_hvx` and `av_hvx` modes establish single-variable causality.
The combined `combined_hvx` mode is the selected local candidate. Static
evidence confirms the intended HVX scatter/shuffle instructions and the
existing HMX path, with no QNN dependency.

## Formal result

Formal evidence was collected at source commit
`403bb7e25079ac785e8c216318f867e2c5c25e8d` in one stable device boot. The
matrix contains 88 records: one audit-on correctness record and five
alternating audit-off repeat-one and repeat-ten records for each of eight
F16F16/W4F16 and control/QK/AV/combined configurations. Both variants retain
their exact output hashes, the full 8 MiB VTCM grant, one FastRPC invocation,
zero intermediate DDR traffic, zero spill/fill, and closed top-level and nested
Attention ledgers.

At repeat ten, the F16F16 control spends 72,867.7 QTimer ticks in QK packing and
72,867.4 in AV packing. The combined candidate reduces those medians to 225.5
and 104.3 ticks. W4F16 changes from 72,865.6 and 72,865.5 ticks to 224.4 and
104.5 ticks. The independent modes reduce only their declared half of the
bottleneck, which confirms that the result is caused by operand construction
rather than an unrelated global schedule change.

The complete Attention median falls by 95.28% for both variants. Equivalent
Host wall improves from 13.123 to 5.212 ms for F16F16 and from 12.830 to
4.906 ms for W4F16 at repeat one. At repeat ten, F16F16 improves from 12.421 to
4.848 ms per block and W4F16 from 12.094 to 4.494 ms per block. The candidate
therefore improves complete-block Host wall by 60.97% and 62.84% respectively
at repeat ten.

After removal of the shared Attention bottleneck, W4F16 is 5.87% faster than
F16F16 at repeat one and 7.29% faster at repeat ten. Its four projection stages
remain 15.11% faster at repeat ten, showing that the existing W4 weight-flow
advantage was previously diluted by a shared scalar packing critical path.

The local gate passes. `adoption_status` remains `pending`, the Selected
Baseline remains unset, and EXP-0023 remains the Provisional Source Parent
until the user explicitly makes an adoption decision.
