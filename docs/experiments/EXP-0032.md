# EXP-0032 — W4F16 Gate/Up critical-path pipeline

## Approval and status

Approved by the user on 2026-08-28 after reviewing the complete-block
EXP-0031 continuation evidence and selecting the W4F16 Gate/Up interval as the
next optimization target. The experiment starts from source commit
`3638b167afd687939786f714fc48987021c02d94`. Using that valid continuation as
the source parent does not accept its pending checkpoint and does not promote
a Selected Baseline.

## Motivation

In the EXP-0031 continuation repeat-ten matrix, Gate/Up occupies 23.397
thousand ticks, or 50.47 percent of the W4F16 complete-block ledger. It is
0.43 percent slower than the paired F16F16 Gate/Up interval despite W4F16
storing only one quarter as many weight bytes. The W4F16 block remains 3.59
percent faster overall only because Down and O projection are 27.57 and 13.65
percent faster respectively; QKV is still 11.45 percent slower.

The tested hypothesis is that the current Gate/Up critical path is governed by
the publication and ownership order between packed-W4 User DMA, HVX expansion,
HMX consumption, and the streaming SwiGLU consumer rather than by compulsory
weight bytes. EXP-0032 changes only that physical schedule.

## Fixed contract

- The Execution Unit remains the complete real Qwen3-1.7B layer-14 block at
  `M=64`, measured at repeat one and repeat ten in one FastRPC invocation.
- F16F16 remains the paired EXP-0031 continuation control. W4F16 retains the
  same W4 values, per-channel scales, FP16 activations, projection arithmetic,
  output layout, standard Softmax, GQA pipeline, QKV readiness overlap,
  streaming SwiGLU, Down projection, and residual arithmetic.
- The candidate may change only the W4F16 Gate/Up packed-weight DMA,
  expansion-region publication, bounded lookahead, HMX command issue, and
  Gate/Up-to-SwiGLU readiness schedule. It may not pre-expand weights in DDR or
  change weight, activation, scale, accumulation, or rounding mathematics.
- Every mode requests and receives exactly 8,388,608 VTCM bytes. Gate, Up,
  activated Gate-by-Up, and Down input remain in VTCM or accelerator state.
  Intermediate DDR, spill/fill, extra FastRPC calls, QNN, and QAIRT are hard
  failures.
- One serialized HMX owner is retained. Existing EXP-0031 Q/K head-readiness
  workers may not race with Gate/Up W4 expansion workers across block phases.
- Candidate output must remain byte-identical to its paired control and pass
  the independent Implementation Reference and non-finite checks.
- Equivalent-scope Host wall is primary. Exclusive ledger intervals and
  overlapping engine-work counters remain distinct.

## Ordered work

### A — Gate/Up dependency audit

Instrument generation-tagged timestamps around packed-bundle DMA completion,
each 32-channel expansion-region publication, HMX command-ready and command-done
handoffs, Gate/Up output-region readiness, and streaming SwiGLU consumption.
Account for the tail after the final useful expansion and identify whether the
23.4-thousand-tick interval is limited by DMA, HVX expansion, HMX ownership, or
consumer backpressure. Instrumentation is audit-only and is excluded from timed
performance collection.

### B — Bounded schedule candidates

Evaluate the smallest schedules supported by the audit. Candidate families may
include an earlier first-region handoff, a different bounded producer
lookahead, Gate/Up interleaving order, or direct region readiness publication
to the existing streaming SwiGLU workers. VTCM slot sizes, the number of HMX
commands, mathematical work, and legal DDR boundaries remain fixed unless a
separate diagnostic proves that changing one is required; such a broader change
must stop for discussion rather than silently expanding scope.

### C — Paired selection gate

Collect audit-on correctness and alternating audit-off repeat-one/repeat-ten
timing in one stable boot. A selected candidate must, at both repeat counts:

- reduce the W4F16 Gate/Up exclusive interval relative to the W4F16 control;
- make the W4F16 Gate/Up interval strictly faster than paired F16F16;
- reduce complete-block W4F16 Host wall without regressing F16F16;
- keep W4F16 strictly faster than F16F16; and
- preserve byte-identical output, exactly 8 MiB VTCM, zero intermediate DDR,
  zero spill/fill, one FastRPC invocation, and one HMX owner.

No fixed percentage target is imposed. If no candidate passes, stop with the
valid diagnostic evidence instead of migrating a local microbenchmark win into
the complete block.
