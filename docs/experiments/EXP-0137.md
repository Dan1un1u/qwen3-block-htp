# EXP-0137 — W4U8 Gate/Up adaptive HVX lead credit

## Decision and hypothesis

EXP-0120 established the current useful W4U8 Gate/Up physical plan: sixteen
compressed and expanded slots, three persistent HVX workers, four-bundle DMA,
32-K-tile streaming regions and batch-eight integer HMX. It reduced Gate/Up by
about 19.5% at repeat ten, but still reports about 6.5 thousand HMX-ready ticks.
Earlier fixed worker-count searches establish both sides of the tradeoff. Two
workers starve HMX, while a fourth worker or a denser decoder increases
simultaneous HVX/HMX contention. The original logical cap also spun surplus
workers and therefore did not remove scheduling pressure.

EXP-0137 tests a bounded feedback scheduler rather than another fixed worker
count. All three workers remain available while the producer lead is low. Once
the number of published but unconsumed 32-tile regions reaches either eight
regions (half of one batch-eight command) or sixteen regions (one full
command), only a third simultaneously entering expansion worker blocks on an
HMX-progress semaphore. It is awakened by real HMX stream consumption and
rechecks the lead. Two expansion workers always remain runnable, activation
tasks are never throttled, and no worker spins in admission backoff.

The hypothesis is that ring16 now provides enough lookahead to sleep surplus
expansion capacity only when it is genuinely ahead, preserving startup and
starvation recovery while reducing HMX contention during steady state.

## Gates

The control is the EXP-0120 ring16/worker3/chain4/stream32 plan emitted by the
current source. Candidate thresholds are eight and sixteen published-minus-
consumed regions. Weight values, asymmetric-U8 qparams, decoder instructions,
task order, DMA bytes and descriptors, region size, HMX batch-eight commands,
tile work, SwiGLU, QKV, Attention, O, Down, residuals and the frozen Public
Common Layer remain fixed.

Static evidence must prove generation-safe published/consumed accounting, a
blocking progress semaphore rather than spin polling, at least two concurrent
expansion contexts, and no throttling of activation tasks. Every cell must be
byte-exact with output `69f22eeb035e5ec5`, preserve all stage audits, request
exactly 8 MiB VTCM, use zero intermediate DDR and spill/fill, one FastRPC
Execution Unit, one HMX owner and no QNN. Physical work and peak VTCM must be
identical apart from declared admission telemetry.

After a bounded three-cell smoke selection, the best candidate advances only
if Gate/Up plus SwiGLU and complete Host wall strictly improve in ordinary and
paired repeat-one and repeat-ten medians over five rotated rounds. PC-027 and
PC-028 are mandatory. A local pass remains pending user decision and cannot
promote a Selected Baseline automatically.
