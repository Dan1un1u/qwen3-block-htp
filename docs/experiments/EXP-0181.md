# EXP-0181 — Attention-side quartet publication for W4U8 decode

## Decision and purpose

EXP-0180 proves that persisting complete four-row V groups in exact HMX carrier
layout reduces QK-Softmax-AV by 19.602%, but append-side publication loses the
end-to-end gate.  Every fourth token currently writes the new raw row to DDR,
reads the whole 512-byte group back to VTCM, converts it, then writes the final
carrier to DDR.  Across 28 layers this raises cache conversion/update by about
467 us/token and creates 112 additional median DMA descriptors.

EXP-0181 tests a new scheduling hypothesis rather than repeating the rejected
path: the fourth raw row is appended normally, the Attention consumer's
already-required tail DMA supplies the group to VTCM, and that loaded copy is
converted once for both immediate AV consumption and persistent publication.
The redundant append-side group read is removed.  Publication happens at the
consumer boundary, where its conversion can share the existing V preparation
work.  The final row of a 32-row segment remains an append-side exception so
the immutable sealed segment is valid before Attention observes it.

## Direct control and candidate

The source implementation parent is the evidence-valid but rejected EXP-0180
commit `073cbd6da8a9f70f75a193298def5484787a6f83`; this is an enabling parent,
not a baseline.  The direct performance control remains the selected-path
EXP-0179 segmented-v4 row-major mutable V tail.

The candidate receives a distinct versioned V6 cache format.  K remains
segmented-v4.  Rows one through three of each group remain raw.  On the fourth
row, append writes only that row; Attention DMA-loads the group, converts it to
the exact HMX carrier in its destination slot, consumes it, and writes that
carrier back to the same persistent tail region.  No duplicate persistent V
tail, full-tile read-modify-write, new HMX work, or changed Attention math is
allowed.

## Gates

Boundary evidence must cross four-row and 32-row transitions and match the
EXP-0179 control byte-for-byte for canonical cache state, consumed AV carrier,
hidden hashes, selected logit codes and tokens.  Telemetry must prove the V6
format, one append per layer/head/token, consumer-side publication only on
eligible group boundaries, no full-tile RMW, and unchanged HMX command/tile and
weight work.  Physical gates remain exact 8 MiB VTCM, one FastRPC per pass, no
QNN, zero timed intermediate/full-logit DDR, zero spill/fill and zero
unattributed ticks.

Five rotated short pairs must improve both QK-Softmax-AV and complete decode
Host wall.  Only then may ten rotated 193-step pairs run.  Direct full-stack
decode token/s is the formal ranking metric; any local pass remains pending
explicit user promotion.
