# EXP-0154 — Fresh corrected-ledger profiling reproduction

## Decision and purpose

The user requested a new run of the latest full-stack profiling on 2026-09-02
after EXP-0153 proved that the old Host/RPC closure bucket was mostly DSP-side
KV-cache carrier conversion. EXP-0154 independently reproduces those corrected
measurements before choosing the next KV-cache optimization.

## Fixed boundary

The immutable source parent is EXP-0153 commit
`21ea7dac13f6da7da3c22bb262fe5a7044ee96e9`. This is a rebuild-and-rerun
experiment only. It may not alter source, kernels, recipe math, schedules,
tensor layouts, the persistent-cache ABI, model packages, instrumentation or
report schema. It reuses the byte-identical F16F16 and W4F16 packages and the
independent exact-reference W4U8 package accepted by EXP-0152.

The run must freshly build and deploy the parent binaries, rerun each package's
layout and device mapping gate, then collect ten rotated three-recipe replay
rounds. Those rounds comprise one M64 prefill and eight continuous decode steps
per recipe and round. Correctness counters, the exact 8 MiB VTCM grant, zero
intermediate DDR, zero spill/fill, one FastRPC per full-stack step, one HMX
owner and no QNN remain mandatory. The corrected complete invocation ledger and
every per-layer ledger must close within 0.1 percent.

There is no speed gate and no baseline-promotion authority. The experiment
exists to establish reproducibility and provide a fresh factual basis for a
separately registered cache-layout optimization.

## Result

EXP-0154 completed on 2026-09-02 using the unchanged source parent
`21ea7dac13f6da7da3c22bb262fe5a7044ee96e9`. A clean Host/DSP rebuild was
deployed to all three device package roots and the local/device binary hashes
matched. The device boot remained
`77ccafe0-8817-4976-bdae-15af5608d4ff`.

Fresh layout and mapping passed for all three recipes. Ten rotated rounds
produced 270 replay records. Correctness, exact 8 MiB VTCM, zero intermediate
DDR, zero spill/fill, one FastRPC per full-stack step, one HMX owner and no QNN
all passed. The maximum complete-invocation residual was 0.01465 percent, the
maximum per-layer residual was 0.06272 percent and the complete ledger retained
zero unattributed ticks.

Relative to EXP-0153, the three prefill medians changed by -0.4168, +0.0199
and -0.3957 percent; decode changed by -0.8054, +0.1247 and -0.3289 percent.
The prefill KV-cache carrier conversion changed by only 0.0121, 0.0039 and
0.0105 percent. The corrected attribution is therefore reproducible rather
than a transient scheduling observation.

W4F16 prefill spends 63,718.2 us, or 53.9 percent of Host wall, converting its
grouped projection/HMX carriers into the persistent row-major K/V cache. W4U8
decode spends 109,957.839 us repacking the complete K prefix and 207,208.750 us
repacking the V prefix into HMX weights; together those two transformations
occupy about 87.3 percent of W4U8 decode wall. The next structural gate should
therefore test a persistent HMX-native K/V-cache ABI, rather than optimize the
small append DMA or the already-fast HMX multiplication.
