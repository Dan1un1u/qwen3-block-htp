# EXP-0177 — Decode W4U8 row-selective AV requantization

## Decision and purpose

The user-authorized continuous decode loop proceeds from the evidence-valid
EXP-0176 candidate without silently promoting it. Formal EXP-0176 attribution
shows that the 28 decode Attention intervals spend about 1.333 ms/token in the
post-HMX AV requantization pass. The current helper scans all 64 physical rows
of every two-head by four-output-tile HMX carrier even though logical-M=1 decode
publishes only row zero to O projection and the persistent cache. Rows 1--63
are padding and cannot affect the token result.

## Direct control and candidate

The control is byte-identical EXP-0176 and applies the centered affine AV
requantization to all 128 HVX vectors in each GQA group. The candidate changes
only logical-M=1 decode: it applies the same exact vector operation to the
first 128-byte vector of each of the eight AV output tiles, covering physical
rows zero through three. Row zero remains mathematically exact; the three
additional rows fill the HVX vector. The remaining padding rows retain their
dead intermediate HMX codes. M64 prefill remains on the full-carrier path.

QK, Softmax, AV HMX commands and tile work, qparams, valid-row arithmetic, QKV,
O batch eight, residuals, MLP, cache, RMSNorm, embedding, LM-head batch sixteen,
F16F16, W4F16 and the no-QNN contract are immutable. Telemetry must distinguish
full-carrier and row-four calls and count the processed vectors.

## Gates

The short gate uses five rotated 17-step pairs and requires byte-exact valid
Attention output, hidden hashes, token IDs and logit codes, requested dispatch,
padding-poison invariance, unchanged QK/AV HMX work, exact 8 MiB VTCM, one
FastRPC/pass, zero timed intermediate/full-logit DDR, zero spill/fill and zero
ledger residual. The candidate advances only if both Attention wall and
complete decode Host wall are lower. Failure stops without the formal run.

A passing candidate completes ten rotated 193-step pairs, the independent
integer head audit and EXP-0163 transformer/cache regression. Direct complete
decode tokens/s is the formal performance authority. A local pass remains
