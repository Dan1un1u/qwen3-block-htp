# EXP-0142 — W4F16 O-projection join-only publication after subgroup4

## Decision and hypothesis

The user's continuing autonomous W4F16/W4U8 specialization loop authorizes
this bounded W4F16 experiment on 2026-09-01. It branches from the locally
passing EXP-0140 source commit
`e75563457293de9430f8c9d20a04ced6147e4737`, preserving the four-context
Gate/Up subgroup4 drain and the corrected Down join-only publication path.

EXP-0140 proved that a nonstreamed projection does not need a visibility fence
after every W4-to-FP16 expansion region when all expansion workers are joined
before HMX consumes the expanded carrier. O projection has the same structural
boundary: its expansion finishes before the single HMX owner consumes the
carrier, and no intermediate consumer observes individual regions.

The hypothesis is that O can use the same final-join publication contract as
Down, eliminating redundant region fences without changing its HMX commands,
carrier, DMA, mathematical output or downstream residual boundary. This is a
new projection boundary, not a repeat of the historical QKV carrier changes,
whose per-head consumers require earlier publication.

## Gates

Control is the complete EXP-0140 candidate: Gate/Up subgroup4 and Down
join-only are enabled while O retains ordered per-region publication.
Candidate changes only O W4-to-FP16 expansion to final-join publication. The
final worker join and visibility barrier remain. Gate/Up, Down, QKV,
Attention, residuals, weights, scales, FP16 activations, HMX batch width, DMA
order and the frozen Public Common Layer remain fixed.

Both cells must produce byte-exact output `f18b9abbe1487231`, zero W4F16
expansion mismatch and zero maximum LSB error. They must request exact 8 MiB
VTCM, retain zero intermediate DDR and spill/fill, one FastRPC Execution Unit,
one HMX owner, no QNN, and identical weight bytes, DMA descriptors, HMX
commands, tile work, carriers and peak plan. Static evidence must show relaxed
publication applies only to O and the already-relaxed Down path, with final
worker join barriers retained.

The candidate passes only if O projection and complete Host wall strictly
improve in ordinary and paired repeat-one and repeat-ten medians over five
rotated rounds, while Gate/Up and Down each regress by no more than 0.5% in any
ordinary or paired median. PC-027 and PC-028 are mandatory. A local pass
remains pending user decision and cannot promote a Selected Baseline.
