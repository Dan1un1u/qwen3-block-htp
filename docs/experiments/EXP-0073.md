# EXP-0073 — Share one prebuilt SOLE Softmax LUT-template bank

## Parent and scope

EXP-0072 is the direct source control. Preserve its six-context residual,
parallel Input RMSNorm, per-GQA-group QK/AV HMX commands, paired-head integer
Softmax, projections, MLP, qparams, layouts, VTCM plan, and every numerical
byte. Change only where the constant SOLE probability LUT-template bank is
built and read.

## Hypothesis

The current paired-head Softmax builds the same fourteen 32-byte SOLE LUT
templates independently inside each of eight GQA-group tasks. The templates
depend only on the fixed SOLE division approximation, not on the group, head,
row, score, or qparam. Building one 448-byte bank before worker dispatch and
letting every group read it from an already-reserved Softmax scratch region
should remove seven redundant builds without changing any row computation.

## Gates

The control retains eight per-group template builds. The candidate builds one
shared read-only bank in the existing `attention_projection` VTCM workspace,
whose Q/K-preparation lifetime has ended and whose O-projection lifetime has
not begun, and passes that pointer to all group tasks. No allocation,
VTCM-plan, scratch-size, Softmax approximation, task scheduling, HMX,
projection, residual, MLP, DMA, or qparam change is allowed.

Final output and audited Input-RMSNorm/QK/probability/AV boundaries must remain
byte-exact to EXP-0072 and the independent implementation reference. Exactly
8 MiB VTCM, zero intermediate DDR, zero spill/fill, one FastRPC Execution
Unit, one HMX owner, and no QNN are mandatory. At repeat one and repeat ten,
the Attention ledger and complete Host wall must strictly improve in ordinary
and paired medians. Seven interleaved paired rounds and PC-027/PC-028 closure
are required.

## Result

The physical and numerical gates passed, but the speed gate failed. The
candidate reduced template construction from eight identical per-group builds
to one shared build while preserving byte-exact final and audited boundary
outputs, 8 MiB VTCM, zero intermediate DDR, zero spill/fill, 176 HMX commands,
and 49,408 HMX tile pairs.

The shared bank nevertheless increased repeat-ten Softmax aggregate work from
13,960.5 to 15,103.3 ticks (+8.19%), Attention wall from 4,694.0 to 4,898.2
ticks (+4.35%), and complete Host wall from 2,065,255.2 to 2,076,588.6 ns
(+0.55%). Repeat one likewise regressed the required Attention target. The
evidence indicates that cheap context-local template construction is preferable
to repeatedly reading one cross-context VTCM bank under this schedule; the
shared bank creates an access-locality or bank-contention penalty larger than
the removed scalar setup work. EXP-0073 is rejected and EXP-0072 remains the
latest locally eligible W4U8 candidate.
