# EXP-0113 — W4U8 Gate/Up generation-release elimination

## Decision and scope

The user authorized a continuing W4F16/W4U8 recipe-specialization loop.
EXP-0113 derives from the locally passing EXP-0112 `single_fence` candidate
without promoting that candidate to a selected baseline. It changes only the
W4U8 Gate/Up stream-region publication instruction sequence. The Frozen
Public Baseline, F16F16, W4F16, QKV, Attention and Down remain unchanged.

## Hypothesis

EXP-0112 showed that one full worker barrier plus a generation store and
address release is faster than barriers inside unpack, bias copy and the
worker. It also showed that removing the worker barrier is not faster at
repeat ten. The remaining publication sequence is therefore:

1. HVX stores expanded S8 weights and the first-region bias in VTCM.
2. One Hexagon full `barrier` completes those stores.
3. The worker performs a volatile generation store.
4. The worker executes `release(address):at` on that generation word.
5. The HMX owner polls the volatile generation, executes its existing full
   barrier, and consumes the corresponding VTCM region.

Because VTCM is directly addressed and the producer full barrier already
orders the payload before the subsequent volatile generation store, while the
consumer full barrier orders payload reads after observing the generation,
the address release may be redundant. EXP-0113 tests this exact claim rather
than retrying the rejected EXP-0112 `release_only` cell.

## Fixed boundary and gates

Control is the EXP-0112 `single_fence` path. The sole candidate keeps relaxed
unpack and bias helpers, the worker full barrier, the volatile generation
store, consumer polling and consumer full barrier, but omits only the producer
address release for W4U8 Gate/Up. It may not change arithmetic, bytes, qparams,
layout, DMA, HMX work, slot count, worker count, other projections or another
recipe.

Both cells must be byte-exact at final output and pass all existing W4U8
intermediate audits with zero mismatch and zero maximum LSB. They retain
exactly 8 MiB VTCM, zero intermediate DDR and spill/fill, one FastRPC execution
unit, one HMX owner and no QNN. Static evidence must prove the exact producer
and consumer instruction contracts. The candidate passes only if Gate/Up plus
SwiGLU and complete Host wall both strictly improve in ordinary and paired
repeat-one and repeat-ten medians over five rounds. Closure requires PC-027
and PC-028, and a local pass does not promote a baseline automatically.

## Formal result and rejection

Source commit `542a0115525e7c00cf5e65530677d4477c062a70` was measured in
five order-rotated rounds at repeat one and repeat ten. Formal evidence is at
`D:/llm_exp/results/qwen3-block-htp/exp0113/20260831T185051Z_542a0115525e_formal`,
with retained binaries at
`D:/llm_exp/models/qwen3-block-htp/exp0113/artifacts/542a0115525e/20260831T185051Z_formal`.

The candidate is byte-exact with control: both preserve output hash
`69f22eeb035e5ec5`, zero mismatches and zero maximum LSB. The physical gate
also passes with exact 8 MiB VTCM, zero intermediate DDR and spill/fill, one
FastRPC execution unit, one HMX owner, and unchanged weight, DMA, HMX,
publication and residency counts.

| Repeat | Gate/Up change | Gate/Up paired | Host change | Host paired |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 1 | -0.599% | -0.223% | +0.645% | +4.970% |
| 10 | +1.151% | +1.188% | +0.119% | +0.516% |

At repeat ten, HMX-ready wait increases 2.69%. Omitting the address release
therefore changes producer-consumer arrival behavior even though the worker
and consumer full barriers preserve the tested numerical result. It fails the
predeclared speed gate and is rejected. EXP-0112 `single_fence` remains the
provisional W4U8 source parent; this experiment does not change a baseline.
