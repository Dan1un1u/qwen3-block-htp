# EXP-0126 — W4F16 head-aligned QKV HMX batch four

## Evidence and hypothesis

The current W4F16 QKV path submits two 32-channel output tiles per HMX command.
One Qwen3 Q/K/V head contains four such tiles, so every head is split across
two HMX commands and two expansion/publication boundaries. Q/K Norm-RoPE cannot
consume a complete head after the first half-command. The candidate expands
and submits four tiles per command, exactly one 128-channel head, while keeping
the accepted direct Crouton carrier. This should halve QKV HMX command and
readiness-publication count, amortize scale setup and synchronization, and
avoid half-head scheduling without changing projection order or arithmetic.

## Variables and gates

The runtime control is the current W4F16 carrier path with QKV HMX batch two.
The candidate changes only Q, K and V projection HMX/expansion batch size to
four. O, Gate/Up and Down retain their current schedules. Weights, scales,
projection order, activation carrier, HMX tile arithmetic, Q/K Norm-RoPE,
Attention, residuals, MLP, legal DDR boundary, FastRPC boundary and QNN status
must not change.

Static evidence must show runtime-selectable QKV batch two/four, exact
four-tile head alignment, unchanged single HMX owner and no QNN. Both cells
must preserve output hash `f18b9abbe1487231`, zero final and QKV operand
mismatch, exact 8 MiB VTCM, zero intermediate DDR and spill/fill, one FastRPC
unit, identical weight bytes and HMX tile-pair work. The candidate must halve
QKV projection HMX commands without changing total projection math.

A bounded diagnostic comparison precedes five order-rotated repeat-one and
repeat-ten rounds for an eligible candidate. The local speed gate requires the
complete QKV plus Q/K Norm-RoPE interval and complete Host wall to strictly
improve in ordinary and paired medians at both repeats. PC-027 and PC-028 are
mandatory. Only the user may promote a Selected Baseline.

## Formal result

The formal source is commit
`500122e068fd85c4a69734b095ba2913aad8c2ca`. Results are retained at
`D:/llm_exp/results/qwen3-block-htp/exp0126/20260831T235041Z_500122e068fd_formal`
and binaries at
`D:/llm_exp/models/qwen3-block-htp/exp0126/artifacts/500122e068fd/20260831T235041Z_formal`.

The candidate initially exposed a real carrier-layout issue: four-column HMX
output has a different Crouton tile order from two consecutive two-column
commands. The final implementation explicitly teaches Q/K Norm-RoPE, V-to-AV
packing and the independent audit both layouts. Formal control and candidate
therefore preserve output hash `f18b9abbe1487231`, zero mismatch, zero maximum
LSB and zero Q, K and V operand-audit mismatch. Both also pass exact 8 MiB
VTCM, zero intermediate DDR and spill/fill, one FastRPC unit, one HMX owner,
identical weight traffic and identical HMX tile-pair work.

The candidate mechanically succeeds in halving QKV commands: complete-block
HMX commands fall 208 to 176 and W4F16 streamed commands 176 to 144. At repeat
ten, HMX compute falls 5.83%, projection HMX wait falls 2.41% and total W4F16
expansion work falls 0.80%. These savings do not shorten the dependency path.
Expansion-pool wait rises 68.91%, first-expansion latency rises 20.79% and the
Q/K preparation-pool tail rises about 363%. QKV plus Q/K preparation therefore
regresses 0.316% at repeat ten and 0.534% at repeat one. Complete Host wall is
effectively flat at repeat ten (+0.015% ordinary, -0.032% paired) and fails its
repeat-one paired gate (+0.163%).

EXP-0126 is rejected. Four-tile batching is too coarse when applied uniformly:
it saves command overhead but delays complete-head weight readiness and
increases contention with asynchronous Q/K preparation. The next bounded
hypothesis is to retain batch two for Q and isolate batch four to V, then K/V,
where command reduction may be obtained without delaying Q-head publication.
The Selected Baseline is unchanged.
