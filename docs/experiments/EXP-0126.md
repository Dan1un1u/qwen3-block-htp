## Evidence and hypothesis

The current W4F16 QKV path submits two 32-channel output tiles per HMX command.
One Qwen3 Q/K/V head contains four such tiles, so every head is split across
two HMX commands and two expansion/publication boundaries. Q/K Norm-RoPE cannot
consume a complete head after the first half-command. The candidate expands
and submits four tiles per command, exactly one 128-channel head, while keeping
the accepted direct Crouton carrier. This should halve QKV HMX command and
readiness-publication count, amortize scale setup and synchronization, and
avoid half-head scheduling without changing projection order or arithmetic.
