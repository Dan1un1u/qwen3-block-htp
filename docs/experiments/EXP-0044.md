## Evidence-driven hypothesis

At repeat ten, EXP-0042 spends 28272.7 qtimer ticks in the exclusive Attention
interval. Its four Attention contexts accumulate about 110.3 thousand worker
ticks: 46874.6 in U8 Q/K Norm plus RoPE, 34870.5 in K operand preparation,
7695.9 in V preparation, 13737.5 in log2 Softmax, and only 3653.6 in QK plus
AV integer-HMX execution. Dividing aggregate work by four gives a 27572.6-tick
ideal lower bound, close to the measured wall interval. The present schedule
is therefore work-bound rather than primarily under-parallelized.

The first hypothesis is that K is scanned and transformed twice. Native-tile
K Norm-RoPE currently gathers every 128-channel row from four 32-channel HMX
tiles, writes U8 back to those tiles, and a later pass rereads it to centre U8
to S8, transpose into the integer-HMX weight carrier, reduce signed column
sums and construct dynamic asymmetric-zero-point bias. Producing the exact
same U8 codes and K carrier in one pass should remove the second scan and its
scalar reduction without changing arithmetic.

The second hypothesis is that EXP-0042 begins Q/K Norm-RoPE only after all QKV
projections finish, whereas the W4F16 control publishes heads during QKV
projection. After the fused operand pass is correct and faster, integer GQA
tasks may consume generation-tagged Q/K head readiness during projection so
that this work overlaps the QKV stage.
