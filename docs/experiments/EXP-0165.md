# EXP-0165 — W4F16 prefill generation-boundary pipeline

## Decision and purpose

The user approved a stepwise optimization of the input/output work newly added
by EXP-0164. The accepted transformer and cache implementation is held fixed.
EXP-0164 measures 82.912 ms for the complete M64 token-to-token prefill while
its earlier transformer-only control measures 54.443 ms on a smaller boundary.
Within the new boundary, embedding and final model RMSNorm together cost about
0.11 ms, while the correctness-first streamed W4 LM head and greedy argmax cost
26.443 ms. EXP-0165 therefore targets the LM-head pipeline first without
assuming that local work reduction equals end-to-end speedup.

## Allowed implementation scope

The source parent is EXP-0164 at
`6a6801193f16fff3ef5e88a25a87f0600b40c18f`. Changes may affect token
embedding, final model RMSNorm, streamed per-output-channel W4 LM-head
execution, online greedy argmax, their VTCM buffer lifetime, and DMA/HVX/HMX
scheduling across those stages. Layers 0-27, projection schedules, Attention,
MLP, persistent KV-cache representation, W4 values, per-output scales,
tokenizer, prompt and expected token sequence are immutable.

The direct control is the complete EXP-0164 token-to-token implementation, not
the smaller EXP-0158 transformer-only replay. Control and candidate must be
measured in rotated pairs. The optimization must not materialize full logits in
the timed path or move DSP work into an unattributed/Host bucket.

## Gates

The selected token, continuous 16-token sequence and decoded text must remain
identical to EXP-0164 and its independent W4F16 reference. The existing
EXP-0158 hidden/cache regression remains mandatory. Physical execution must
retain exactly 8 MiB VTCM, one FastRPC call per pass, no QNN, zero inter-layer
hidden or timed full-logit DDR traffic, zero spill/fill and a closed additive
ledger.

The adoption metric is the median paired improvement of repeat-ten complete
M64 Prefill Host wall. The candidate must be strictly faster than the EXP-0164
control. The aggregate embedding/final-norm/LM-head ledger must also decrease as
a diagnostic. Decode latency is reported because the same output head is used,
but it is not the promotion metric for this prefill-scoped experiment.

## Decision state

Approved by the user on 2026-09-03 and completed on the same date. Evidence is
valid, all local gates pass, and adoption remains pending because only the user
may promote a baseline.

## Implementation

The formal binary retains the EXP-0164 control and adds an isolated candidate.
The candidate replaces the scalar per-tile greedy scan with an HVX FP16 maximum
reduction and a stable scalar lane resolution only for a tile pair that creates
a new global maximum. It also increases the streamed LM-head output batch from
two to eight. The eight-way batch does not request more VTCM: after the
transformer finishes, dead Gate/Up and intermediate regions are phase-overlaid
for compressed weights and HMX scale blocks, while the existing expanded-weight
region is reused. LM-head HMX commands decrease from 2,374 to 594 while the
4,748 output tiles, model weights, scales and arithmetic remain unchanged.

## Formal result

The formal evidence is
`D:/llm_exp/results/qwen3-block-htp/exp0165/20260903T122920Z_c005032a6d27_formal`.
Ten rotated control/candidate sessions contain 160 selected-token checks. The
candidate has zero token mismatches against the independent W4F16 reference and
the selected FP16 logit bits are identical to the control at every step. All
sessions decode to `低比特量化（Low-Bitwidth Quantization）在大模型推理中`, and
the earlier nine-step transformer/cache regression also passes.

For M64 prefill, median Host wall decreases from 82,846.693 to 65,669.610 us;
the paired median speed improvement is 26.442% and throughput rises from
772.511 to 974.576 tokens/s. The streamed W4 LM-head plus argmax interval falls
from 26,484.010 to 9,396.536 us. Embedding and final RMSNorm remain essentially
unchanged at about 0.106 ms in total. Decode, retained only as a report-only
metric for this experiment, decreases from 112,594.748 to 95,349.049 us/token.

The candidate keeps exactly 8 MiB VTCM with an unchanged 8,171,008-byte peak,
one FastRPC call per pass, no QNN, no inter-layer or timed full-logit DDR tensor,
zero spill/fill and zero unattributed ledger ticks. LM-head DDR reads remain
156,190,208 bytes, confirming that the gain comes from scheduling, command
amortization and vectorized argmax rather than hidden data removal.

The experiment therefore passes its correctness, semantic, physical, ledger,
local-diagnostic and prefill wall-time gates. `adoption_status` remains
`pending`, and no Selected Baseline is changed automatically.
