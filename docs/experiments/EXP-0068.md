# EXP-0068 — Scale the persistent W4U8 Attention HVX context pool

## Parent and scope

EXP-0065 is the direct source control. Preserve its W4U8 projections, paired
Q/K Norm-RoPE kernels, fused QK requant-Softmax loading, SOLE LUT templates,
batched QK/AV HMX commands, residuals, MLP, qparams, layouts, and all numerical
bytes. Change only the number of persistent HVX contexts participating in Q/K
preparation and GQA Attention.

## Profiling basis and hypothesis

At repeat ten, EXP-0065 spends about 555 microseconds in QKV plus Q/K
Norm-RoPE and 260 microseconds in QK-Softmax-AV, versus 439 and 141
microseconds for the selected W4F16 comparator. The three asynchronous Q/K
preparation workers still leave about 3.74k qtimer ticks of join wait, while
the eight GQA groups are also distributed over only the main context plus
three workers.

The hypothesis is that extending the persistent pool to four or five workers,
in addition to the main HVX context, can shorten both tails. Unlike changing
task arithmetic, HMX batching, or tensor layouts, this tests whether the
remaining penalty is parallel-domain occupancy. All HMX submissions remain
serialized through the same owner.

## Bounded search and gates

The control uses four total Attention HVX contexts: the main DSP thread and
three persistent workers. Diagnostic candidates use five and six total
contexts. Three interleaved repeat-ten rounds select the lower combined
QKV-plus-Attention ledger, with complete Host wall as the deterministic
tie-breaker. Only that candidate receives the seven-round formal comparison
against the four-context control.

Final output, QK, probability, and AV boundaries must remain byte-exact to
EXP-0065 and the independent reference. HMX commands and tile pairs, W4 weight
traffic, qparams, mask, tasks, and residual/MLP schedules cannot change.
Exactly 8 MiB VTCM, zero intermediate DDR, zero spill/fill, one FastRPC
Execution Unit, one HMX owner, and no QNN are mandatory. Telemetry must prove
that every requested persistent worker was created and acquired an HVX lock.

At repeat one and repeat ten, the combined QKV-plus-Attention ledger and
complete Host wall must strictly improve in ordinary and paired medians.
Seven interleaved paired rounds and PC-027/PC-028 closure are required.
