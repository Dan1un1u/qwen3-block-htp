# EXP-0189 — Direct-n decode row-four SwiGLU

## Decision and purpose

The user authorized continued decode optimization after promoting EXP-0188.
EXP-0188 is the immutable direct control at source commit
`17068d8b7d505381a4c524a7ad7cfe21c9f5abad`.  Its formal evidence separates the
17.357 ms/token Gate/Up plus SwiGLU interval into about 9.601 ms of serial
direct-n Gate/Up projections and 7.756 ms of serial SwiGLU work.  The SwiGLU
loop scans all 64 physical HMX rows even though logical-M1 decode consumes only
row zero.

EXP-0171 previously rejected a four-row SwiGLU candidate because activation
tasks were embedded in the Expanded-S8 DMA-expand-HMX producer pipeline:
removing overlapped LUT work changed producer pacing and exposed additional HMX
lifetime and ready waits.  EXP-0188 is a new scheduling mechanism satisfying
that experiment's reopen condition.  Direct-n has no timed W4-to-S8 expansion,
and both blocking Gate and Up HMX calls finish before the standalone SwiGLU
loop begins.  The candidate therefore tests whether removing dead-row work now
reduces the actual serial critical path without perturbing projection pacing.

## Direct control and candidate

One runtime-selectable binary compares `full64`, the exact EXP-0188 behavior,
with `decode_row4`, which applies the unchanged U8 LUT SwiGLU arithmetic to the
first 128-byte HVX vector (four physical rows) of every 64-by-32 Gate/Up output
tile only when logical-M1 direct-n decode is active.  Row zero is the sole
logical output; rows one through 63 are padding.  M64 prefill remains full64.

The experiment may change only this decode SwiGLU row count, its dispatch,
padding audit, telemetry and experiment scripts.  It may not change weights,
scales, qparams, LUT contents, valid-row math, projection/HMX commands, weight
DMA, Attention, cache, other nonlinear operators, LM head, F16F16, W4F16 or the
no-QNN architecture.

## Gates

Correctness requires byte-exact valid-row middle activations, complete hidden
hashes, log2 codes, selected tokens and cache state against the same-binary
full64 control, with the existing independent integer references retained.
Padding poison must prove rows four through 63 cannot affect any valid output.

The physical gate remains exactly 8 MiB VTCM, zero timed intermediate DDR,
zero spill/fill, one FastRPC call per pass, one HMX owner, no QNN, unchanged
direct-n projection work and zero timed QKVO/MLP W4-to-S8 expansion.  Telemetry
must prove candidate decode uses four rows and control/prefill use 64 rows.

A five-pair short gate precedes ten rotated formal pairs.  Every pair runs one
real M64 prefill followed by 192 continuous decode steps through valid length
256.  The candidate must produce strictly higher directly measured full-stack
decode tokens/s and lower Gate/Up plus SwiGLU wall than the EXP-0188 control.
Failure of correctness, the physical contract or the short performance gate
stops the experiment before formal profiling.
