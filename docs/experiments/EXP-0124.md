# EXP-0124 — W4U8 QKV producer/consumer ring

## Decision

Approved by the user's continuing autonomous W4F16/W4U8 specialization loop
on 2026-09-01. This experiment branches from the accepted EXP-0120 W4U8
ring16 source parent. It does not modify the frozen Public Common Layer.

## Hypothesis

The current W4U8 Q/K/V path stages one four-output batch, expands all four W4
tiles on the orchestration thread, waits the preceding HMX command, and then
submits the next command. A four-batch producer/consumer ring can let User DMA,
parallel HVX W4-to-S8 expansion, integer HMX consumption, and already accepted
Q/K Norm-RoPE preparation progress concurrently. Moving the sixteen Q/K/V
batches through one bounded pipeline should reduce the additive QKV interval
without changing projection or Attention mathematics.

## Variables and boundary

The control is EXP-0120's head-aligned batch-four Q/K/V implementation. The
candidate uses four compressed, expanded and bias slots, one ordered DMA
producer, one HMX owner consuming batches in Q-then-K-then-V order, and one
long-lived mixed HVX epoch. Runtime candidate cells assign one, two, or three
of the five persistent HVX workers to W4 expansion; the remaining workers begin
Q/K preparation and expansion workers join that preparation queue after their
last expansion task. Existing head-ready publication remains batch/head
aligned.

No W4 values, per-channel scales, qparams, activation carrier, HMX tile math,
Q/K Norm-RoPE arithmetic, Attention implementation, O/MLP/residual path,
public configuration, legal DDR boundary, or FastRPC boundary may change.
Phase-retired block buffers may be overlaid as extra QKV ring slots, but the
declared peak and every lifetime must be audited.

## Gates

Static evidence must prove exact control/ring selection, four bounded slots,
one DMA producer, one HMX owner, mixed worker partition, Q/K head publication,
and no QNN dependency. Every candidate cell must preserve final output hash
`69f22eeb035e5ec5` and all existing W4U8 stage audits with zero mismatch and
zero maximum LSB error.

Formal device evidence requires exactly 8 MiB VTCM, zero intermediate DDR,
zero spill/fill, one FastRPC execution unit, one HMX ownership domain, and
unchanged weight bytes, DMA descriptor count, HMX tile-pair work, qparams and
logical outputs. The only permitted physical differences are the declared
ring residency, worker-command cadence and overlap/wait counters.

A candidate is locally eligible only if the complete QKV plus Q/K Norm-RoPE
ledger interval and complete Host wall strictly improve over control in both
ordinary and paired repeat-one and repeat-ten medians. After a bounded
diagnostic matrix, the best eligible cell receives five order-rotated formal
rounds. PC-027 and PC-028 are mandatory. A local pass cannot promote a
Selected Baseline without explicit user approval.

## Formal result and rejection

Source commit `95fadff5930d9081678ad99bb62ef84849ee1897` was measured
for five order-rotated repeat-one and repeat-ten rounds. Formal evidence is
archived at
`D:/llm_exp/results/qwen3-block-htp/exp0124/20260831T231805Z_95fadff5930d_formal`,
with retained binaries at
`D:/llm_exp/models/qwen3-block-htp/exp0124/artifacts/95fadff5930d/20260831T231805Z_formal`.

All four runtime cells preserve output hash `69f22eeb035e5ec5`, zero final
mismatch and zero maximum LSB error. Control and ring3 both receive exactly
8 MiB VTCM, use zero intermediate DDR and spill/fill, retain one FastRPC unit
and one HMX owner, and execute identical weight bytes, DMA descriptors and
HMX tile-pair work. Ring3 closes 32 head-aligned batches, 128 expansion tasks,
24 Q/K head publications and one long HMX dispatch per block.

The structural ring succeeds locally at its intended boundary. At repeat ten,
QKV plus Q/K Norm-RoPE falls from 409.96 to 390.07 microseconds, a 4.85%
reduction, and complete Host wall falls from 1604.19 to 1585.27 microseconds,
a 1.18% reduction. The paired medians improve by 5.01% and 0.79%. At repeat
one, QKV similarly improves by 6.32% and its paired median by 6.03%, but the
ordinary complete-Host median changes from 2022.03 to 2026.09 microseconds,
a 0.20% regression, despite a 0.81% paired improvement.

The strict four-way speed gate therefore fails on repeat-one ordinary Host
wall and the candidate is rejected rather than promoted. This is a boundary
success but not a complete-block eligibility pass. EXP-0120 remains the
provisional W4U8 source parent. Reopening should preserve the ring while
removing its long Q/K preparation tail: ring3 spends about 262 microseconds
waiting for the preparation pool because three expansion workers do not join
that queue until all V expansion is complete.
