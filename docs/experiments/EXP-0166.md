# EXP-0166 — W4F16 streamed LM-head overlap

## Decision and purpose

The user promoted EXP-0165 and approved continued optimization on 2026-09-03.
EXP-0165 reduces the complete M64 prefill to 65.670 ms and the streamed W4
LM-head plus greedy argmax to 9.397 ms. Embedding and final model RMSNorm total
only about 0.106 ms, so EXP-0166 targets the remaining LM-head critical path.

## Hypothesis and allowed scope

The direct control is the Selected EXP-0165 batch-eight, phase-overlaid LM head
with HVX argmax. The candidate may change only scale residency, compressed
weight DMA prefetch, W4-to-FP16 expansion buffering and worker assignment, HMX
consumption order, synchronization and phase-overlaid VTCM lifetime inside the
streamed LM head. The objective is to overlap DMA, HVX expansion and HMX work
rather than execute their aggregate work largely in series.

Embedding, final RMSNorm, argmax mathematics and tie behavior, transformer
layers 0-27, Attention, MLP, KV cache, W4 values, per-output scales, tokenizer,
prompt and generated sequence are immutable. Weight DDR bytes, output tile
count and HMX FP16 tile-pair count must remain unchanged.

## Gates

The candidate must preserve selected tokens, selected FP16 logit bits and the
complete 16-token sequence against EXP-0165 and the independent W4F16
reference. The existing EXP-0158 transformer/cache regression remains
mandatory. Physical execution retains exactly 8 MiB VTCM, one FastRPC call per
pass, no QNN, zero inter-layer or timed full-logit DDR tensors and zero
spill/fill.

The adoption metric is the rotated-pair repeat-ten complete M64 prefill Host
wall against EXP-0165. Streamed LM-head wall must also decrease with complete
DMA, HVX, HMX, wait and command attribution. Decode is reported but is not the
promotion metric.

## Decision state

Approved by the user on 2026-09-03. Execution and evidence are pending.
