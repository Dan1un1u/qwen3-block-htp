# EXP-0166 — W4F16 streamed LM-head overlap

## Decision and purpose

The user promoted EXP-0165 and approved continued optimization on 2026-09-03.
EXP-0165 reduces the complete M64 prefill to 65.670 ms and the streamed W4
LM-head plus greedy argmax to 9.397 ms. Embedding and final model RMSNorm total
only about 0.106 ms, so EXP-0166 targets the remaining LM-head critical path.

## Hypothesis and allowed scope

The direct control is the Selected EXP-0165 batch-eight, phase-overlaid LM head
with HVX argmax. The candidate may change only scale residency, compressed
weight DMA prefetch, W4-to-FP16 expansion buffering and worker assignment, HMX
consumption order, synchronization and phase-overlaid VTCM lifetime inside the
streamed LM head. The objective is to overlap DMA, HVX expansion and HMX work
rather than execute their aggregate work largely in series.

Embedding, final RMSNorm, argmax mathematics and tie behavior, transformer
layers 0-27, Attention, MLP, KV cache, W4 values, per-output scales, tokenizer,
prompt and generated sequence are immutable. Weight DDR bytes, output tile
count and HMX FP16 tile-pair count must remain unchanged.

## Gates

The candidate must preserve selected tokens, selected FP16 logit bits and the
complete 16-token sequence against EXP-0165 and the independent W4F16
reference. The existing EXP-0158 transformer/cache regression remains
mandatory. Physical execution retains exactly 8 MiB VTCM, one FastRPC call per
pass, no QNN, zero inter-layer or timed full-logit DDR tensors and zero
spill/fill.

The adoption metric is the rotated-pair repeat-ten complete M64 prefill Host
wall against EXP-0165. Streamed LM-head wall must also decrease with complete
DMA, HVX, HMX, wait and command attribution. Decode is reported but is not the
promotion metric.

## Decision state

Approved by the user on 2026-09-03 and completed on the same date. Formal
evidence is valid and all local gates pass. Adoption remains pending because
only the user may promote a candidate to baseline.

## Implementation

The experiment retained EXP-0165 as generation mode 4 and evaluated three
isolated schedules. Fine-grained region streaming successfully overlapped HMX
with expansion but increased HVX expansion time enough to erase most of the
gain. A DMA/HVX-only schedule was faster. The selected generation mode 7 uses
a coarser two-slot pipeline: while HMX consumes group `i-1`, HVX expands group
`i` and DMA prefetches compressed group `i+1`; HVX argmax for group `i-1`
runs while HMX starts group `i`.

Raw per-output-channel scales are copied once into the dead Up buffer. Their
HMX scale carriers are generated with an HVX batched conversion in dead
attention-phase VTCM. Compressed weights, expanded weights, scale blocks and
logit tiles each use two phase-overlaid slots. This preserves the eight-output
tile HMX command size and does not increase the fixed 8 MiB VTCM allocation.

## Formal result

Formal evidence is
`D:/llm_exp/results/qwen3-block-htp/exp0166/20260903T_exp0166_8e0dcf8_formal`

Ten rotated control/candidate session pairs contain 160 generated-token checks.
Every candidate token and selected FP16 logit bit is identical to EXP-0165 and
to the independent W4F16 reference. All sessions generate
`低比特量化（Low-Bitwidth Quantization）在大模型推理中`, and the accepted

Median M64 prefill Host wall decreases from 65,793.646 to 62,974.714 us; the
paired median speed improvement is 4.433%, and throughput increases from
972.738 to 1,016.281 tokens/s. The LM-head interval decreases from 9,451.224
to 6,701.953 us, a 41.02% speed increase. Decode decreases from 95,541.920 to
92,598.101 us/token, a report-only paired median improvement of 3.148%. The
complete 16-pass session decreases from 1,498,162.238 to 1,451,748.411 us.

The candidate keeps LM-head DDR reads at 156,190,208 bytes and HMX work at
4,748 output tiles, 594 LM-head commands, and the same full-stack 3,070,720

LM-head weight-prefetch wait is only 118.854 us and HMX tail wait is 129.349
us; these counters show that the coarse pipeline hides almost all DMA and HMX
tail latency even though their lifetime counters overlap the additive wall.

The physical contract passes with exactly 8,388,608 VTCM bytes requested and
acquired, unchanged 8,171,008-byte planned peak, one FastRPC invocation per
