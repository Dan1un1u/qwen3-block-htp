# EXP-0208 — Direct-W4 QKV batch16 single-descriptor DMA

## Decision and purpose

EXP-0208 retains EXP-0207's mathematically and physically valid Down
single-descriptor path as an enabling parent. Q, K and V keep the accepted
batch16 command granularity so head publication timing does not change. The
only candidate change is to submit each 524288-byte packed-W4 QKV group as one
weight descriptor followed by its bias descriptor, rather than synchronously
retiring two 262144-byte halves.

There are 224 QKV groups per generated token. The candidate should therefore
remove exactly 224 DMA descriptors and the associated half-group waits while
preserving every HMX command, tile pair, weight byte and Q/K preparation
boundary. This isolates DMA granularity from EXP-0206's confounded batch32 head
readiness change.

## Gates and decision state

One binary compares QKV split3 with weight1+bias1. Both cells use Down batch4
single-DMA and otherwise reproduce EXP-0207. Correctness requires byte-exact
QKV preparation, hidden state, cache, selected codes and tokens. Physical gates
remain exact 8 MiB VTCM, zero timed intermediate DDR, zero spill/fill, one
FastRPC, one HMX owner, no QNN, zero explicit W4 expansion, identical weight
bytes/HMX commands/tile pairs and exactly 224 fewer DMA descriptors. Five
rotated pairs must improve QKV wall and full decode token/s before ten formal
pairs. The user's consecutive local-win/E2E-regression stop gate remains at
zero.
