# EXP-0069 — Decouple Q/K preparation and GQA Attention HVX domains

## Parent and scope

EXP-0068 is the direct source control. Preserve its W4U8 arithmetic, qparams,
tensor layouts, six-context persistent pool, HMX commands and tile pairs,
weight flow, residuals, MLP, SOLE LUT templates, and all numerical bytes.
Change only how many already-created persistent HVX contexts may claim GQA
Attention tasks after Q/K preparation has joined.

## Profiling basis and hypothesis

EXP-0068 shows opposite context-scaling responses in the two phases. Six total
contexts reduce Q/K preparation pool wait by about 69% and shorten the QKV
ledger by about 24%. However, compared with four contexts, repeat-ten aggregate
Q/K Norm-RoPE work rises 5.6%, Softmax work rises 7.2%, and Attention pipeline
wait rises 145.6%. The additive Attention ledger remains nearly unchanged.

The hypothesis is that Q/K preparation needs all six contexts, whereas GQA
Attention benefits from a smaller active domain. A persistent six-context pool
can therefore retain the preparation gain while allowing only four or five
total contexts to claim Attention tasks. HMX ownership remains serialized and
no worker is created or destroyed inside the timed block.

## Bounded search and gates

The control uses six contexts for both Q/K preparation and GQA Attention.
Candidates keep six preparation contexts and restrict Attention to four or
five contexts. Three interleaved repeat-ten rounds select the lowest combined
QKV-plus-Attention ledger, with complete Host wall as the tie-breaker. Only
that candidate receives the seven-round formal comparison against the
six/six control.

Final output, QK, probability, and AV boundaries must remain byte-exact to
EXP-0068 and the independent reference. HMX commands/tile pairs, tasks,
qparams, layouts, DMA traffic, VTCM plan, projection, residual, and MLP work
cannot change. Exactly 8 MiB VTCM, zero intermediate DDR, zero spill/fill, one
FastRPC Execution Unit, one HMX owner, and no QNN are mandatory. Telemetry must
prove six persistent contexts were created and locked and must separately
report the active Q/K-preparation and GQA-Attention context counts.

At repeat one and repeat ten, the Attention ledger, combined
QKV-plus-Attention ledger, and complete Host wall must strictly improve in
ordinary and paired medians. Seven interleaved paired rounds and PC-027/PC-028
closure are required.

## Result

The bounded search selected six contexts for Q/K preparation and five active
contexts for GQA Attention. The candidate is mathematically and physically
valid: final output, QK, probability, and AV hashes remain byte-exact to
EXP-0068 with zero implementation-reference mismatches and zero maximum LSB
error. Both paths create and lock all five persistent workers, retain exactly
8 MiB VTCM with a 5,306,080-byte peak plan, perform 192 HMX commands and
49,408 HMX tile pairs, and have identical DMA traffic, zero intermediate DDR,
zero spill/fill, one FastRPC Execution Unit, one HMX owner, and no QNN.

Restricting Attention to five contexts does reduce the targeted internal work.
At repeat ten, aggregate Softmax work falls 5.611%, Attention pipeline wait
falls 55.556%, and the additive Attention ledger falls 1.814%. The combined
QKV-plus-Attention interval falls only 0.484%, however, because QKV changes by
+0.272%. Complete Host wall improves only from 2,140,140.6 to 2,137,234.4 ns
(-0.136%).

At repeat one, ordinary Host wall is nearly flat (-0.016%), while the paired
Host-wall median regresses by 0.962%. Although Attention falls 1.689% and the
combined interval falls 0.880%, the strict repeat-one complete-wall gate
fails. EXP-0069 is therefore rejected. EXP-0068 remains the latest eligible
W4U8 candidate and provisional source parent. Formal evidence is retained at
`D:/llm_exp/results/qwen3-block-htp/exp0069/20260830T093306Z_8d68aa65e459_formal`.
