# EXP-0065 — Precompute SOLE probability-LUT templates per GQA group

## Parent and scope

EXP-0064 is the direct source control. Preserve its paired full-width QK
requantization, per-head QK2/AV4 HMX command batching, Q/K Norm-RoPE, V packing,
qparams, causal mask, residual equations, projection schedules, and MLP. Change
only how the existing SOLE probability lookup-table entries are produced.

## Hypothesis

For SOLE division mode, each of the sixteen probability entries depends on the
row sum only through its highest set bit and the following bit. For 1 to 64
valid values, the sum always includes at least one maximum log2 weight, so only
fourteen `(leading, next)` templates are reachable. Building those templates
once in each GQA group's existing reusable VTCM scratch can replace two scalar
sixteen-entry LUT loops on every row. Selecting templates must reproduce the
original LUT bytes exactly and must not introduce a DDR-backed table.

## Gates

Control and candidate must be selectable in one binary. Final output and the
actual QK, probability, and AV audit boundaries must remain byte-exact to
EXP-0064 and the independent implementation reference. Exactly 8 MiB VTCM,
zero intermediate DDR, zero spill/fill, one FastRPC Execution Unit, one HMX
owner, and no QNN are mandatory. HMX commands, tile pairs, weights, qparams,
mask semantics, and group scheduling remain unchanged.

At repeat one and repeat ten, Softmax work, the Attention ledger, and complete
Host wall must strictly improve in ordinary and paired medians. Seven
interleaved paired rounds and PC-027/PC-028 closure are required.

## Result

The candidate passed every correctness and physical gate. Final output, QK,
probability, and AV boundaries remain byte-exact with zero mismatches and zero
maximum LSB error. Both variants acquired exactly 8 MiB VTCM, used 5,306,080
bytes at peak, performed zero intermediate DDR transfers and zero spill/fill,
and retained one FastRPC Execution Unit, one HMX owner, 192 HMX commands, and
49,408 HMX tile pairs per block.

At repeat one, Softmax improved from 13,153 to 12,958 ticks (-1.483%), the
Attention ledger from 5,047 to 4,994 ticks (-1.050%), and complete Host wall
from 2,677,969 to 2,667,552 ns (-0.389%). At repeat ten, Softmax improved from
13,179.9 to 12,926.3 ticks (-1.924%), Attention from 5,069.7 to 4,987.9 ticks
(-1.614%), and Host wall from 2,291,161.5 to 2,288,052.1 ns (-0.136%). Paired
medians preserve every required direction, although the complete-block gain is
near the noise floor.

EXP-0065 passes its strict local gate and becomes the latest W4U8 candidate,
but it is only a marginal optimization and does not become a Selected Baseline
without explicit user promotion. Formal evidence is retained at
`D:/llm_exp/results/qwen3-block-htp/exp0065/20260830T081948Z_5ad3c0fbcf0c_formal`.
