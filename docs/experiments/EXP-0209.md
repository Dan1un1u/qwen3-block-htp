# EXP-0209 — Direct-W4 mixed Q32 and K/V16 batching

## Decision and purpose

EXP-0206 showed that uniform QKV batch32 reduces QKV wall and has a positive
median E2E effect, but it delayed head readiness enough to make the five-pair
result unstable. EXP-0209 isolates the useful portion: Q uses batch32 while K
and V remain batch16. This reduces Q projection command and DMA-chain count but
preserves the K/V publication granularity consumed by Attention.

The candidate inherits EXP-0207's valid Down single-DMA path. Relative to its
uniform batch16 control, QKV command groups should fall from 224 to 168 per
token and weight-DMA descriptors from 2614 to 2502, while HMX tile pairs,
weight bytes and all arithmetic remain identical.

## Gates and decision state

Correctness requires byte-exact QKV preparation, hidden state, cache, selected
codes and tokens. Physical gates remain exact 8 MiB VTCM, zero timed
intermediate DDR, zero spill/fill, one FastRPC, one HMX owner, no QNN, zero
explicit W4 expansion, identical weight bytes/HMX tile pairs and the exact
command/descriptor deltas above. Five rotated pairs must improve QKV wall and
full decode token/s before ten formal pairs. The user's consecutive
local-win/E2E-regression streak remains zero.

Approved and registered on 2026-09-05 under the continuous decode-loop
authorization. Evidence is not yet available and no baseline changes
automatically.

## Result

The short gate measured 45.297773 versus 45.469364 decode tok/s (+0.378807%)
with all five pairs faster. The ten-pair formal run measured 45.057685 versus
45.334056 tok/s (+0.613370% median); QKV plus Q/K preparation fell from
2587.596 to 2500.438 us/token. QKV commands fell 224 to 168 and total DMA
descriptors 2614 to 2502, with identical HMX tile work and weight bytes.
Correctness and all physical gates passed.

One formal pair regressed by 0.500112%, so the strict all-pairs gate fails even
though 14 of 15 short-plus-formal pairs favored the candidate. The experiment
is rejected as standalone adoption but retained as an evidence-valid enabling
parent under PC-030. Median E2E improved, so the user's local-win/E2E-regression
streak remains zero. Evidence is retained under
`D:/llm_exp/results/qwen3-block-htp/exp0209/`.
