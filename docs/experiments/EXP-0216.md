# EXP-0216 — Full-stack M64 prefill direct-W4 QKV/O integration

## Decision and purpose

EXP-0215 proved that eliminating explicit HVX W4-to-S8 expansion from all M64
Gate/Up/Down projections improves complete prefill throughput by 14.357% while
preserving every mathematical and physical gate.  Its formal profile leaves
QKV plus Q/K preparation at about 7.77 ms and O projection at about 4.84 ms;
QKVO still performs about 9.21 ms of overlapping explicit expansion work.

EXP-0216 retains the evidence-valid EXP-0215 MLP implementation and applies
the same HMX direct `weight.n` packed-W4 carrier to M64 Q, K, V and O across all
28 layers.  The direct control is the complete EXP-0215 path.  The candidate
may change only the M64 QKVO carrier, safe batch granularity, phase-local VTCM
slots and telemetry required to prove that physical contract.  Decode remains
the EXP-0211-derived direct-W4 path and must not change.

## Required gates

Correctness requires byte-identical complete prefill hidden output, KV cache,
selected logit codes and tokens to EXP-0215.  Physical gates are exactly 8 MiB
VTCM, zero timed intermediate DDR, zero spill/fill, one FastRPC execution unit,
one HMX owner, no QNN or CPU fallback, direct packed-W4 HMX for all 28-layer
M64 Q/K/V/O and Gate/Up/Down projections, and zero explicit Transformer
projection W4-to-S8 expansion in the candidate.

Performance ranks only by alternating-order, directly measured complete M64
prefill Host wall and token/s.  Decode and LM head are preservation controls.
If this candidate fails, the open-ended user-authorized prefill loop continues
with a genuinely different physical hypothesis; no candidate is promoted to a
Selected Baseline without explicit user approval.
