# EXP-0082 — Arithmetic-sign-extension W4U8 decoder

## Parent and scope

EXP-0079 is the direct source parent and control. EXP-0081 is a rejected
sibling; its Q/K-preparation-worker assistance is not inherited. Preserve the
EXP-0079 persistent MLP workers, dependency-driven integer Attention,
arithmetic, qparams, layouts, exact 8 MiB VTCM, zero intermediate DDR, one
FastRPC Execution Unit, one HMX owner and no QNN path.

## Evidence-driven hypothesis

Every current W4U8 projection expands a packed byte by masking and shifting its
two nibbles, running two `vlut32` signed-value lookups, shuffling the results
into HMX carrier order and storing two vectors. The signed four-bit value can
instead be formed exactly as `(nibble ^ 8) - 8`. An HVX arithmetic decoder can
therefore replace both vector lookups with byte-wise XOR and subtraction while
leaving the final shuffle and stores unchanged.

The hypothesis is that ordinary vector ALU operations issue more efficiently
than the two lookup operations and avoid LUT-path pressure. Because the same
decoder is used by Q/K/V/O, Gate/Up and Down, a real improvement should reduce
aggregate W4-to-S8 expansion work and complete block wall latency rather than
move work between QKV and Q/K preparation.

## Decision

EXP-0082 is completed with valid evidence, `local_gate: fail`, and
`adoption_status: rejected`. On V79, two `vlut32` lookups form a better decoder
than the longer mask/shift/XOR/subtract dependency chain. EXP-0079 remains the
provisional W4U8 source parent and latest locally eligible candidate; the
user-selected W4U8 baseline remains EXP-0055.
