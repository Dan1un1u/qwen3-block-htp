# EXP-0183 — Partial prepared-session VTCM-native mutable K tail

## Decision and purpose

EXP-0182 proves that repeated reconstruction of the mutable V-cache tail was
an avoidable long-decode cost: preserving its native HMX carrier in the
prepared-session VTCM improves Attention by 32.082% and direct decode by
3.091%.  The remaining segmented K tail still follows the old path on every
token: read all active logical rows from DDR, recenter and scatter them into an
HMX operand, then discard that operand.  Formal EXP-0182 evidence attributes
about 461.824 us/token to K packing in addition to K-tail cache reads.

The complete native K-tail atlas does not fit beside the accepted V-tail atlas
under the exact 8 MiB grant.  EXP-0183 therefore tests the maximum simple
capacity-safe subset: seven of eight KV heads for every one of 28 layers.  It
is a physical feasibility and speed experiment, not a change to Attention
math or cache quantization.

## Physical design

The direct control is the evidence-valid EXP-0182 V7 candidate at commit
`803c4a104b2b16bfd8b0cf5d456a76ea8738b4cb`.  The candidate retains V7 and adds
a versioned K-cache format whose external DDR allocation remains byte-for-byte
segmented-v4.  For KV heads zero through six, a prepared-session VTCM atlas
stores one 4,096-byte native K weight carrier plus 32 compact signed correction
words per layer/head.  The invariant conversion half of the HMX bias carrier
is regenerated when copied into the Attention slot, avoiding 128 persistent
bytes per head.  The resulting atlas is 827,904 bytes and fits above the
EXP-0182 peak with no overlap.  Head seven remains the unchanged DDR row-tail
control inside the same invocation.

Each append still updates the canonical DDR row journal.  The new K row is
also scattered once into its cached native output lane and its correction word
is updated.  Attention copies cached native weights/corrections for seven
heads, while only head seven reads and reconstructs the logical K tail.  At a
32-row boundary the cached carrier seals the immutable DDR K segment and its
mutable atlas is reset for the next segment.  V-cache handling, Softmax, AV,
all projection/MLP/LM-head kernels, qparams and generated-token feedback remain
unchanged.

## Gates

A 34-step boundary replay must prove exact K/V external cache bytes, consumed
QK and AV carriers, hidden hashes, selected logit codes and tokens across
quartet and segment boundaries.  Telemetry must prove exactly seven cached and
one fallback KV head per layer, one cached-row update per eligible head/token,
no cached K-tail DDR read, correct segment sealing, no full-carrier RMW, exact
VTCM accounting and padding-poison invariance.

Five rotated 17-step pairs must lower both QK-Softmax-AV and complete decode
Host wall in 5/5 pairs before formal profiling.  Formal evidence requires ten
rotated 193-step pairs, higher direct decode token/s, 193/193 independent
integer-head agreement and the EXP-0163 regression.  Exact 8 MiB VTCM, one
FastRPC per pass, no QNN, zero timed intermediate/full-logit DDR, zero
spill/fill and zero unattributed ticks remain mandatory.  A local pass remains
pending explicit user promotion.
