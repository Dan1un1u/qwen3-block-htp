# EXP-0139 — W4U8 QKV main-context prep assist

## Decision and hypothesis

EXP-0120 is the latest valid W4U8 Gate/Up local candidate and EXP-0124
demonstrated a useful four-slot QKV producer/consumer ring. The ring shortened
repeat-ten QKV plus Q/K Norm/RoPE by 4.852% and complete Host wall by 1.180%,
but failed its strict repeat-one ordinary Host gate. EXP-0125 then showed that
handing an expansion worker to QK preparation early is not the missing
mechanism: it delays V expansion, increases HMX-ready and producer-slot waits,
and regresses repeat ten.

In the ring implementation the FastRPC main context finishes feeding all QKV
weight DMA batches and then blocks first on the HMX owner and later on the QK
preparation pool. EXP-0139 keeps all three expansion workers and both existing
QK preparation workers unchanged, but lets the already-resident main context
claim remaining QK preparation tasks after the final QKV DMA batch has been
published and before it waits for HMX completion. This adds no worker, steals
no expansion capacity, and uses time that is currently a pure completion tail.

The hypothesis is that main-context assistance closes the QK preparation tail
without delaying V readiness or changing the QKV ring's HMX schedule.

## Gates

Three cells are measured from one source: the EXP-0120-compatible sequential
QKV control, the EXP-0124 ring with three expansion and two preparation
workers, and the same ring with main-context preparation assist. Ring slots,
DMA order and bytes, expansion workers, preparation workers, projection
weights, asymmetric-U8 qparams, integer HMX commands and tile work, QK
Norm/RoPE arithmetic, downstream Attention, MLP, residuals, and the frozen
Public Common Layer remain fixed. The only declared difference is that the
main context may execute already-ready QK preparation tasks after all QKV DMA
batches have been published.

Static evidence must show that main assistance runs only after the DMA feed is
complete, before the main HMX wait, and without changing either pool worker
role. Dynamic evidence must show at least one main-context preparation task in
every assisted block and no main-context task in either control. Every cell
must produce byte-exact output `69f22eeb035e5ec5`, pass all stage audits,
request exact 8 MiB VTCM, retain zero intermediate DDR and spill/fill, one
FastRPC Execution Unit, one HMX owner, no QNN, and identical weight bytes, DMA
descriptors, HMX commands and tile work, qparams, carriers, and peak VTCM plan.

The assisted ring passes only if QKV plus Q/K Norm/RoPE and complete Host wall
strictly improve over both the sequential and unassisted-ring cells in
ordinary and paired repeat-one and repeat-ten medians over five rotated
rounds. PC-027 and PC-028 are mandatory. A local pass remains pending user
decision and cannot promote a Selected Baseline automatically.
