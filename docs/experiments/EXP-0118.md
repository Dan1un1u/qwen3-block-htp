# EXP-0118 — W4U8 preemptible worker-assisted Q expansion

## Decision and scope

The user authorized the continuing W4F16/W4U8 recipe-specialization loop and
routine autonomous recovery. EXP-0118 derives from the provisional combined
EXP-0112 source parent and keeps its `single_fence` W4U8 Gate/Up path. It
changes only Q-projection W4-to-S8 expansion scheduling. K/V/O, Attention
arithmetic, Q/K Norm-RoPE arithmetic, MLP, qparams, weights, public common
layer, DMA boundary and HMX commands remain unchanged.

## Prior evidence and new hypothesis

EXP-0081 proved that opportunistically lending idle Q/K-preparation workers to
Q expansion reduced QKV expansion by about 9.1% and QKV wall by about 4.3%, but
each helper became non-preemptible for one complete 64-K-tile output tile.
When a head dependency arrived during that interval, Q/K preparation join
pressure rose about 19.5% and the strict repeat-one Host gate failed.

EXP-0118 tests a distinct scheduling hypothesis: expose one output tile as
four 16-K-tile regions. An idle preparation worker may claim one region, then
must re-check its Q/K dependency before claiming another. The main thread also
drains unclaimed regions and waits only for the current batch generation. This
retains parallel W4 expansion while bounding helper non-preemptibility to one
quarter of the EXP-0081 interval. Stage A applies only to Q, where early worker
idle time exists and EXP-0081 already demonstrated local leverage.

## Gates

Control is the byte-identical EXP-0112 W4U8 path; candidate is runtime
selectable. Static evidence must show a fixed 16-K-tile assistance region,
generation-safe region claims, one-region-per-dependency-check worker cadence,
and no QNN. Device evidence must show positive assisted region and tile counts,
all twelve paired Q/K preparation tasks exactly once, and no early dependency
read. Final output plus existing input-norm, score, probability and AV audits
must remain byte-exact with zero mismatch and zero maximum LSB.

Exact 8 MiB VTCM, zero intermediate DDR and spill/fill, one FastRPC execution
unit, one HMX owner, unchanged weight and boundary bytes, DMA descriptors, HMX
commands, tile pairs and peak VTCM are hard gates. QKV projection and complete
Host wall must both strictly improve in ordinary and paired repeat-one and
repeat-ten medians over five rotated rounds. PC-027 and PC-028 are mandatory.
Stage B may extend the same mechanism to K/V only if Stage A passes; a local
pass remains a candidate until the user adopts it.

## Outcome

Stage A was implemented at source commit
`e806cce87c60e188c37c0e08acf5caaa72d74b67`; validator normalization was
corrected without changing the runtime at
`cc091ab2bd095d8d9d4a7de084104d3b0fccde09`. Formal evidence is archived at
`D:/llm_exp/results/qwen3-block-htp/exp0118/20260831T205015Z_e806cce87c60_formal`
and runtime artifacts at
`D:/llm_exp/models/qwen3-block-htp/exp0118/artifacts/e806cce87c60/20260831T205015Z_formal`.

Static, correctness, execution and physical gates passed. Control and
candidate produced byte-exact output `69f22eeb035e5ec5`, zero mismatch and zero
maximum LSB, retained exact 8 MiB VTCM, zero intermediate DDR and spill/fill,
one FastRPC unit and one HMX owner. The candidate assisted about 100 of 320 Q
expansion regions per block, never exceeded 16 K tiles per assistance turn,
reduced Q expansion wall by about 5.2% and QKV wall by about 3.7%–3.8%.

The bounded turn fixed the prior EXP-0081 long non-preemptibility problem but
did not remove shared-resource coupling: Q/K preparation pool wait increased
about 6.3%. Repeat-one complete Host improved 0.70% ordinary and 0.84% paired.
Repeat-ten improved 0.72% by ordinary medians but regressed 0.41% by paired
median. The strict repeat-ten Host gate therefore failed. Stage B was not
opened; EXP-0118 is rejected and does not change a baseline. The result remains
useful evidence that smaller assistance regions make Q expansion and QKV
consistently faster, while a finer preemption/granularity choice is required
to make complete-block latency stable.
