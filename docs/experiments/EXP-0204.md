# EXP-0204 — Direct-W4 decode O-projection batch8 command granularity

## Decision and purpose

EXP-0204 returns to the fastest evidence-valid EXP-0201 source. The direct-W4
decode O projection currently groups four output tiles per HMX command. The
candidate groups eight tiles, seeking to halve command and weight-DMA
descriptor granularity while preserving packed-W4 bytes, arithmetic and HMX
tile work.

The experiment is intentionally decode-only. Both cells retain the accepted
O-to-Gate prefetch, QKV batch16, interleaved Gate/Up production and asynchronous
U8 SwiGLU consumer from EXP-0201. M64 prefill remains unchanged.

## Gates

Correctness requires byte-exact hidden, cache, selected-code and token outputs.
The physical contract remains exact 8 MiB VTCM, zero timed intermediate DDR,
zero spill/fill, one FastRPC per pass, one HMX owner, no QNN and zero explicit
decode W4-to-S8 expansion. Packed-W4 bytes and HMX tile pairs must be
identical, while O command and DMA descriptor counts must decrease.

Five rotated pairs must show both lower O-projection wall time and higher
complete decode token/s. Only then may ten rotated 193-step formal pairs run.
The user-defined consecutive local-optimum/E2E-regression gate applies.

## Decision state

Approved and registered on 2026-09-05 under the user's continuous decode-loop
authorization.

The five-pair short gate and ten-pair formal gate both passed on source commit
`79319bf1d5f41953d522b582300d7e80d0975605`. Formal decode improved from
43.389197 to 43.997668 token/s, or 1.402%, with all ten pairs faster. O wall
fell from 1649.606 to 1356.107 us/token. O commands halved from 448 to 224 and
total weight-DMA descriptors fell from 3958 to 3510, while packed-W4 bytes and
HMX tile pairs remained identical. Outputs were byte-exact and every physical
gate passed. The candidate is evidence-valid and pending user promotion; it
becomes the parent for the next decode-loop experiment but does not
automatically alter an official baseline.
