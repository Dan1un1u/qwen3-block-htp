# EXP-0207 — Direct-W4 Down batch4 single-descriptor DMA

## Decision and purpose

EXP-0207 returns to the evidence-valid EXP-0205 candidate. EXP-0205 proved that
Down batch4 is valid once its two packed-W4 carriers no longer alias persistent
decode-cache state. Its retained safety implementation still splits each
393216-byte weight group into two transfers, synchronously retires the first
half, and then launches the second half plus bias. That produces three DMA
descriptors per group and leaves avoidable serialization in the 4.016-ms Down
interval.

The project already executes 512-KiB and one-MiB packed-W4 weight descriptors
in direct-N QKV, Gate/Up and LM-head paths. EXP-0207 therefore tests the corrected
physical hypothesis: with the lifetime-safe Expanded-S8 slots retained, submit
the complete 393216-byte Down weight group as one descriptor plus one bias
descriptor. The candidate should reduce Down DMA descriptors from 1344 to 896
per token and eliminate the diagnostic split wait without changing arithmetic,
bytes, HMX commands or tile work.

## Gates and decision state

One binary compares EXP-0205 split3 with native two-descriptor batch4. All
weights, qparams, QKV batch16, Attention, O batch8, O-to-Gate prefetch,
Gate/Up batch32, asynchronous SwiGLU, Down HMX batch4, cache, LM head and M64
prefill remain identical. Correctness requires byte-exact hidden, cache,
selected codes and tokens. Physical gates remain exact 8 MiB VTCM, zero timed
intermediate DDR, zero spill/fill, one FastRPC, one HMX owner, no QNN, zero
explicit expansion, identical weight bytes/HMX commands/tile pairs and exactly
448 fewer DMA descriptors. Five rotated pairs must improve Down wall and full
decode token/s before ten formal pairs. The user's consecutive
local-win/E2E-regression stop gate remains active.

Approved and registered on 2026-09-05 under the continuous decode-loop
authorization. Evidence is not yet available and no baseline changes
automatically.

## Result

The physical hypothesis is valid. The short gate measured 44.462290 versus
45.374196 decode tok/s (+2.050965%) and all five rotated pairs favored the
candidate. The ten-pair formal run measured 44.395916 versus 45.144910 tok/s
(+1.687079% median); Down fell from 4001.986 to 3589.334 us/token, DMA
descriptors fell from 3062 to 2614 per token, and all arithmetic, weight bytes,
HMX commands and tile pairs were preserved. Correctness, exact 8 MiB VTCM,
zero timed intermediate DDR, zero spill/fill, one FastRPC, one HMX owner and no
QNN all passed.

One formal pair regressed by 1.384478%, so the experiment fails its deliberately
strict all-pairs performance gate despite a positive median and 14 wins across
15 short-plus-formal pairs. It is not promoted and is rejected as a standalone
adoption, but retained as an evidence-valid enabling parent under PC-030. This
does not increment the user's local-win/E2E-regression streak because median
E2E improved. Evidence is retained under
`D:/llm_exp/results/qwen3-block-htp/exp0207/`.
