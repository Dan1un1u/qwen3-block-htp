## Evidence and hypothesis

EXP-0124 proves that a four-slot ring with three parallel W4 expansion workers
reduces the complete QKV plus Q/K Norm-RoPE interval by 4.85% at repeat ten.
It misses the complete-block gate because all three expansion workers remain
assigned through the V tail and only then join Q/K preparation. Ring3 therefore
spends about 262 microseconds in the final preparation-pool wait.

The candidate retains three expansion workers for all Q and K tiles. At the
Q/K-to-V boundary, one or two workers atomically leave the expansion queue and
join the already active Q/K preparation queue. The remaining two or one worker
finishes V expansion. This exchanges a bounded V-ready delay for earlier Q/K
Norm-RoPE parallelism without changing tile order, arithmetic, head-ready
publication, one-HMX ownership or the four-slot VTCM ring.

capacity merely moves the tail from Q/K preparation into V readiness and ring

expansion capacity. The Selected Baseline is unchanged.
