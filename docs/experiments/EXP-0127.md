# EXP-0127 — W4F16 projection-selective QKV HMX batch four

## Decision

The user authorized the continuing W4F16 and W4U8 specialization loop and
explicitly authorized routine branch, synchronization and worktree repair
without interruption. EXP-0127 is the bounded successor to rejected EXP-0126.
It does not promote a baseline.

## Hypothesis

EXP-0126 proved that four-tile HMX commands reduce command and matrix-engine
overhead, but applying them uniformly to Q, K and V delays Q-head publication
and grows the Q/K preparation tail. The benefit may be retained by keeping Q
at batch two while applying batch four only where head readiness is less
critical. V has no Q/K Norm-RoPE publication dependency; K is tested separately
to determine whether it can share the larger command without recreating the Q
tail.

## Variants

The three runtime-selectable cells use identical W4F16 weights, scales,
activation, projection order, attention and MLP mathematics:

- `qkv_hmx_batch2_control`: Q, K and V use two output tiles per HMX command.
- `v_hmx_batch4_candidate`: Q and K remain batch two; V uses batch four.
- `kv_hmx_batch4_candidate`: Q remains batch two; K and V use batch four.

The source parent is EXP-0126 commit
`500122e068fd85c4a69734b095ba2913aad8c2ca`, because that source contains the
audited four-column Crouton carrier readers. The logical W4F16 performance
control remains EXP-0111/EXP-0120.

## Gates

Static and correctness gates require exact runtime projection selection,
byte-identical complete output `f18b9abbe1487231`, zero final and Q/K/V operand
mismatch, and unchanged W4 values, scales and projection work. The physical
gate remains exact 8 MiB VTCM, zero intermediate DDR, zero spill/fill, one
FastRPC Execution Unit, one HMX owner and no QNN.

The local speed gate requires both complete QKV plus Q/K Norm-RoPE and complete
Host wall to strictly improve in ordinary and paired medians at repeat one and
repeat ten. A bounded diagnostic chooses whether either candidate is eligible
for five rotated formal rounds. PC-027 and PC-028 are mandatory. Only the user
may promote a Selected Baseline.

All three cells produce output hash `f18b9abbe1487231`, zero final mismatch,
zero maximum LSB and zero independent Q, K and V operand mismatch. They retain
exact 8 MiB VTCM, zero intermediate DDR and spill/fill, one FastRPC Execution
Unit, one HMX owner, identical W4 traffic and identical HMX tile-pair work.

The command reduction is real. V-only changes complete-block HMX commands from
208 to 200 and streamed W4F16 commands from 176 to 168; K+V changes them to 192
and 160. At repeat ten, QKV plus Q/K preparation improves 0.222% for V-only and
0.427% for K+V. Complete Host wall, however, is effectively flat and slightly
worse in the ordinary median: +0.0022% and +0.0032%. At repeat one both
candidates improve Host wall, but QKV itself regresses 0.273% and 0.062%.

Larger K/V commands still coarsen expansion readiness. At repeat ten the
expansion-pool wait grows 19.57% for V-only and 36.95% for K+V, while first
expansion grows 7.88% and 14.19%. The saved HMX command overhead is therefore
absorbed before it can shorten complete block latency. Both candidates fail the
strict speed gate and the batch-two control remains selected. Further batch
width search is closed unless accompanied by a new readiness or expansion
mechanism. The Selected Baseline is unchanged.
