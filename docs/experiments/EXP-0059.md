# EXP-0059 — Pair Softmax rows on top of the vectorized V carrier

## Decision

EXP-0058 passed every local gate and becomes the provisional source parent. EXP-0056 previously proved that paired 64-element Softmax rows are byte-exact and reduce Softmax work, but it missed the strict repeat-one Host-wall gate by 0.106%. It is not inherited as a source branch.

## New hypothesis

EXP-0058 removes another 2.15k V-pack worker ticks and shortens the Attention interval. In this changed critical-path context, pairing the two Q-head rows in each GQA group may now expose its already-proven Softmax and Attention savings at the complete-block wall boundary. This is an integration re-evaluation on a different direct control, not a reinterpretation of EXP-0056 as passing.

## Candidate

Keep EXP-0058's exact `vgather` plus five-stage `vdeal` V carrier, qparams, projection schedules, QK and AV HMX, residual, and MLP paths. Apply only the EXP-0056 byte-exact two-head Softmax row packing. Control and candidate remain selectable in one binary.

## Gates

Final output and QK/probability/AV boundaries must remain byte-exact to EXP-0058 and the independent reference. Formal runs require exactly 8 MiB VTCM, zero intermediate DDR, zero spill/fill, one FastRPC execution unit, one HMX owner, and no QNN. Softmax work, Attention ledger, and complete Host wall must strictly improve at repeat one and repeat ten. Failure closes this paired-row hypothesis under the current Attention contract.

## Result

Implementation commit `0c37ee2bab5540fc9a580563d4dc5eb0d95f42c2`
preserved the complete EXP-0058 `vgather` plus five-`vdeal` path and made
independent versus paired Softmax selectable in one binary. Final output,
QK, probability, and AV hashes were byte-exact; requested and acquired VTCM
was exactly 8 MiB; intermediate DDR and spill/fill remained zero.

Paired Softmax reduced Softmax work by 9.929% at repeat one and 9.657% at
repeat ten. The Attention ledger improved by 5.098% and 5.079%, and ordinary
Host-wall medians improved by 0.541% and 0.557%. However, the interleaved
repeat-one paired Host-wall median regressed by 1.288%. Repeat ten passed all
three speed targets, but repeat one did not.

## Final decision

`local_gate=fail`, `adoption_status=rejected`. EXP-0056 is not reinterpreted
and paired-row Softmax is closed under the current Attention contract. The
latest eligible W4U8 candidate remains EXP-0058.
