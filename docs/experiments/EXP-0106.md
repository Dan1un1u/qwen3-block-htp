# EXP-0106 — Integrated fair-comparison and fastest W4U8 baselines

## Decision and motivation

The user directed that the latest valid optimization results be consolidated
instead of remaining isolated on sibling experiment branches. EXP-0084 remains
the accepted common-source fair-comparison authority, while EXP-0099 is the
fastest complete W4U8 executable measured so far. EXP-0093, EXP-0095 and
EXP-0104 each passed their local correctness, physical and speed gates but were
never integrated with EXP-0099.

EXP-0106 creates one clean source state that can emit both outputs required by
the project: a same-source three-recipe fair matrix and the fastest validated
W4U8 physical plan. It begins from EXP-0099 and integrates the byte-exact
four-row native residual shuffle from EXP-0093, four-row native Softmax carrier
shuffle from EXP-0095, and quarter-tiled paired Q/K Norm-RoPE kernel from
EXP-0104. EXP-0098 is superseded by EXP-0099 and EXP-0105 is rejected; neither
may enter the candidate.

## Integration cells

The implementation retains runtime-selectable cells in one binary so component
interaction is measured rather than inferred from old percentages:

1. the accepted EXP-0084 W4U8 fair control;
2. the EXP-0099 Down-batch-four fastest control;
3. EXP-0099 plus the EXP-0095 Softmax carrier;
4. the preceding cell plus the EXP-0093 residual shuffle;
5. the preceding cell plus the EXP-0104 Q/K Norm-RoPE kernel.

F16F16 and W4F16 remain byte-identical to their EXP-0084 paths. Their existing
FP16 residual, Softmax and Norm-RoPE implementations already express the
corresponding full-width physical optimizations; U8-only tile shuffles are not
forced into incompatible FP16 layouts. All three recipes are rebuilt from the
same source and remeasured in one stable device session.

## Fixed numerical and physical contract

The Execution Unit remains the real Qwen3-1.7B layer-14 complete block at
M=64. W4 values, scales, asymmetric-U8 qparams, projection arithmetic, log2
Softmax approximation, causal mask, residual equations and all external tensor
contracts remain fixed. Candidate stages must be byte-exact at final output and
at the established Input-RMSNorm, QK, probability and AV audit boundaries, with
zero mismatches and zero maximum LSB.

Every cell requires one FastRPC invocation, exactly 8,388,608 acquired VTCM
bytes, zero intermediate DDR and spill/fill, one HMX owner, no QNN, and the same
weight bytes, DMA descriptors and HMX tile arithmetic as its declared parent.
Static evidence must prove the retained Down batch-four command, four-row
Softmax shuffle, four-row residual shuffle and quarter-tiled paired Q/K kernel,
with no rejected EXP-0105 dual-head helper path.

## Performance and adoption gates

Each incremental integration cell first passes static, correctness and physical
gates. Repeat-one and repeat-ten order-rotated runs then select the lowest valid
complete Host-wall cell; a component that regresses the integrated critical
path is omitted even if its isolated experiment passed. The final W4U8 candidate
must strictly improve complete Host wall over the EXP-0099 fastest control in
ordinary and paired medians at both repeat counts.

The final same-source F16F16, W4F16 and W4U8 matrix must preserve the accepted
output hashes and report complete PC-027 and PC-028 profiling. F16F16 and W4F16
must show no material regression relative to EXP-0084; any change beyond one
percent requires diagnosis rather than baseline promotion. Only the user may
promote the resulting fair-comparison and fastest W4U8 baselines.

## Result

Running. Source commit `4deccaeb7a914a38398cfc7061916f3ba859ce52`
successfully integrates the EXP-0099 Down batch-four path, EXP-0095 Softmax
carrier shuffle, EXP-0093 Residual shuffle and EXP-0104 paired Q/K quarter
tiling. Its first formal collection passed W4U8 correctness, physical-contract
and incremental speed gates; the integrated repeat10 cell was 1762.828 us
versus 1866.990 us for the EXP-0099 control.

Before promotion, audit found that the F16F16 and W4F16 fair cells in this
collection used four FP16 RMSNorm contexts, while the accepted EXP-0084 common
physical plan requires three. The measurements were within one percent of the
accepted controls, but they are not the exact declared fair plan. Promotion is
therefore intentionally withheld. The run is retained as preliminary W4U8
integration evidence at
`D:/llm_exp/results/qwen3-block-htp/exp0106/20260831T130757Z_4deccaeb7a91_formal`;
EXP-0106 remains active until the run script is corrected and the entire
same-boot formal matrix is recollected.
