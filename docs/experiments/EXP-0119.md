# EXP-0119 — W4U8 Q-expansion assistance granularity

## Decision and scope

The user authorized a continuing W4F16/W4U8 recipe-specialization loop and
routine autonomous recovery. EXP-0119 follows the valid negative evidence from
EXP-0118. It keeps the accepted public layer and provisional EXP-0112 W4U8
`single_fence` parent unchanged and modifies only the maximum Q-projection
W4-to-S8 region an otherwise-idle Q/K-preparation worker may execute before it
must re-check its head dependency.

## Evidence and hypothesis

EXP-0118 bounded assistance to 16 K tiles and consistently reduced Q expansion
about 5.2% and QKV wall about 3.8%, but Q/K-preparation pool wait still rose
about 6.3%; repeat-ten paired Host regressed 0.41%. The remaining causal
variable is the bounded non-preemptible interval. EXP-0119 compares 16-, 8- and
4-K-tile assistance turns against the byte-identical EXP-0112 control. The
hypothesis is that 8 or 4 K tiles can reduce dependency-service delay enough to
make complete Host latency stable while retaining useful parallel expansion.

## Gates

All cells are runtime selectable from one binary. K/V/O, Attention arithmetic,
Q/K Norm-RoPE arithmetic, qparams, weights, layouts, DMA, HMX commands, MLP,
residual and the public common layer remain unchanged. Static and device
telemetry must prove the selected maximum region, positive worker assistance,
all twelve paired Q/K tasks exactly once and closed K-tile accounting.

Final output and existing W4U8 stage audits must remain byte-exact. Exact 8 MiB
VTCM, zero intermediate DDR and spill/fill, one FastRPC unit, one HMX owner and
unchanged traffic, DMA descriptors, HMX work and peak VTCM are hard gates. A
candidate is eligible only if QKV projection and complete Host wall strictly
improve in ordinary and paired repeat-one and repeat-ten medians over five
rotated rounds. PC-027 and PC-028 are mandatory. Only the user may promote an
eligible candidate to baseline.
