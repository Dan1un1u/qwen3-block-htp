## Problem and hypothesis

The formal EXP-0042 repeat-ten profile reports about 13.17 thousand qtimer
ticks per block outside its named exclusive stage ledger, roughly nine percent
of the complete DSP interval. This is an instrumentation defect: the interval
must be divided into adjacent, mutually exclusive activities before it can
support optimization decisions.

The working hypothesis is that the gap is composed of existing W4U8 boundary
work and scheduler handoffs that are currently measured only by overlapping
engine counters or not bounded at all. Candidate categories include activation
pack/unpack, projection setup and teardown, worker publication/join, HMX-owner
handoff, and final output preparation. No category is assumed to be the cause
until adjacent timestamps prove it.

## Fixed implementation contract

- The Execution Unit remains the real Qwen3 layer-14 complete block at `M=64`.
- The EXP-0042 arithmetic, qparams, tensor layouts, VTCM plan, one-HMX-owner
  schedule, worker counts, command grouping and DMA descriptors remain fixed.
- One FastRPC invocation, an exact 8 MiB VTCM grant, zero intermediate DDR,
  zero spill/fill and no QNN path remain mandatory.
- Source changes may add compact timestamps, counters, field serialization and
  offline validation only. They may not change an operator, numerical result,
  dependency, buffer lifetime, prefetch distance, tile size or schedule.
- The instrumented and uninstrumented modes must produce the same output hash
  and the same declared engine-work counts.

## Measurement method and gates

The DSP records an ordered Block Timing Ledger from the first timed block
activity through the final output boundary. Every ledger entry is bounded by
adjacent timestamps and assigned exactly once. Engine Work Counters remain
diagnostic and are not added to the exclusive ledger.

Formal repeat-one and repeat-ten runs compare the instrumented candidate with
an uninstrumented EXP-0042 control in paired order. Completion requires:

- top-level exclusive intervals reconcile to the complete DSP interval;
- every nested ledger used in the explanation reconciles to its parent;
- median unattributed time is no more than 0.1 percent of the complete DSP
  interval at repeat one and repeat ten;
- output hash, implementation-reference result and physical gates remain
  unchanged;
- instrumentation overhead is reported and must not exceed one percent of Host
  wall at repeat ten for the attribution build to be used in later profiling.

If the overhead gate fails, the attribution result may still be retained as
diagnostic evidence, but the instrumentation may not remain enabled in the
normal performance path. EXP-0043 has no speed gate and cannot change the
Selected Baseline.
