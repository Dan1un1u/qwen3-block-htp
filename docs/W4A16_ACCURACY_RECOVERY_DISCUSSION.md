Before or alongside an approved comparison, use a bounded fixed development slice to check actual packed A0 software against DSP and localize quality sensitivity by restoring FP16 for attention/MLP/head module families in software. Local weight MSE alone is not the selection target, effects are not additive, and old EXP222 LM-head ablation involved an older transformer and small qbh set. A full FP16 head is a diagnostic, not an assumed deployable fix.

If calibration provides little recovery, prioritize a host-only symmetric W4 group128 versus per-output-channel control before committing DSP changes. This tests quantization-format freedom; it is ordinary group quantization, not upstream LPBQ and not reuse of folded weights. With oneFP32scale/128weights, metadata is0.25bit/weight (~6.25% of raw4bit weight payload), excluding alignment/headers and existing per-row metadata. W4F16 can conceptually expand grouped weights before FP16 HMX, but DMA/scale lookup/VTCM and speed costs require measurement. Grouping requires explicit amendment/approval of PC012 before implementation. Do not infer a quality improvement or zero runtime cost without data.

An alternative within the per-channel carrier is AWQ-style activation-informed input-channel equalization: W x = (W D)(D^-1 x). This adds input-coordinate freedom beyond existing output-row scale selection. It needs legal Qwen3 norm/residual/GQA/nonlinearity compensation and exported FP16 invariance checks; it is not arbitrary gamma folding and cannot blindly commute through SiLU. More expensive learnable clipping/equivalent transforms or quantization-aware training are later options. Learned rotation alone and fixed-code row-scale training have not shown independent improvement here and are lower priority.

Use separate calibration/training, fixed development/checkpoint validation, and milestone testing. EXP229 primary is now an exposed regression benchmark; repeated monitoring must not be described as fresh independent acceptance or be used to select hyperparameters. Select candidates on development data, freeze before final testing, and retain a fresh untouched final set. Preserve pairedNLL/PPL and5%overall/10%stratum gates; short answers remain auxiliary.
